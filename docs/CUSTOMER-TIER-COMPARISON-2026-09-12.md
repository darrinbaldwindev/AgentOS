## Tier messages

### Free

> **AgentOS helps you.**

Try the AgentOS way of working with useful limits. Make use of free/included AI, meet the full AgentOS team and see how planning, execution, governance and assurance work together.

### $39 Co-worker

> **AgentOS works with you.**

For regular users who want more capacity, more connected tools, repeatable workflows and a limited taste of scheduled/Night Shift work without moving all the way to Operator.

### $99 Operator — Recommended

> **AgentOS can work for you, on your schedule.**

For people who want AgentOS to take on more of the workload: broader scheduling, recurring jobs, greater persistence, higher concurrency, larger queues, more integrations and the fullest normal-plan Night Shift experience.

> **$99 a year — less than $2 a week.**

## Savings story

Preferred wording:

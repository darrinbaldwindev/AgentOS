# AgentOS Segmented Landing Pages — 2026-09-12

**Status:** MARKETING DIRECTION / TESTABLE COPY
**Scope:** Customer-facing landing-page variants using the current commercial source of truth. These variants do not change the product, pricing, governance, or entitlement model.

## Shared rules

All variants must preserve:
- One AgentOS product.
- Active ladder: Free -> $39/year Co-worker -> $99/year Operator -> optional add-ons/BYO AI.
- $99 Operator is the recommended destination.
- $99/year may be described as less than $2/week.
- AgentOS may be positioned as designed to make the most of free, local and paid AI.
- Do not claim guaranteed savings, guaranteed time recovery, universal provider support, or fully implemented Night Shift/remote execution without evidence.
- Jack, Henry, Green and PRS trust/safety concepts are not premium safety paywalls.

## Life-stage guidance

Age may be used as a campaign-testing variable, but customer-facing pages should be framed around goals/life stage rather than age labels.

Suggested message mapping:
- 18–24: study, first work, affordability, organisation
- 25–34: career, side projects, busy life
- 35–49: work + family load, delegation, recurring work
- 50–64: simplicity, usefulness, professional/home organisation
- 65+: clarity, confidence, everyday usefulness, larger/cleaner presentation

Avoid patronising language such as “AI for seniors.”

## Test matrix

For each page measure:
- hero-to-scroll rate
- primary CTA rate
- pricing-section reach
- Free vs $39 vs $99 click distribution
- Operator selection rate
- referral/source conversion
- message comprehension
- objections collected

Do not interpret click-through as proof of product capability or savings.

# AgentOS Affiliate Program — Legal Review Checklist

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Status:** PRE-LAUNCH REVIEW CONTROL

## Purpose

Give owner and qualified counsel a concise list of decisions and legal checks required before the AgentOS Affiliate Program is opened publicly.

## A. Contracting and jurisdiction

- [ ] Confirm legal entity that operates the affiliate program.
- [ ] Confirm registered/business address used in legal notices and commercial email where required.
- [ ] Confirm governing law.
- [ ] Confirm dispute venue/process.
- [ ] Confirm whether local consumer/small-business mandatory rights override chosen law in relevant markets.

## B. Commercial schedule

- [ ] Confirm commission rate.
- [ ] Confirm one-time vs recurring commission.
- [ ] If recurring, confirm duration.
- [ ] Confirm 90-day attribution mechanics.
- [ ] Confirm first-click / last-click / other attribution model.
- [ ] Confirm treatment of upgrades within attribution window.
- [ ] Confirm existing customers / returning customers.
- [ ] Confirm refund, chargeback and fraud reversal rules.
- [ ] Confirm payout cadence.
- [ ] Confirm minimum payout threshold.
- [ ] Confirm payment methods/currency/fees.
- [ ] Confirm tax documentation and any withholding/reporting obligations.

## C. Advertising / endorsement compliance

- [ ] Approve mandatory disclosure wording for AU/US/UK.
- [ ] Confirm use of “Ad” / “Affiliate” wording across supported channels.
- [ ] Confirm video disclosure requirements.
- [ ] Confirm policy for gifted/free access and sponsored creator campaigns.
- [ ] Confirm affiliate monitoring/training obligation and enforcement process.
- [ ] Confirm process for stale or misleading claims.

## D. Email, SMS and messaging

- [ ] Confirm affiliates may only market to their own lawfully obtained audience unless separately approved.
- [ ] Prohibit scraped, harvested and purchased lists by default.
- [ ] Require compliance with local sender identification and unsubscribe rules.
- [ ] Confirm no “refer a friend by entering their email” mechanic without separate privacy/spam review.
- [ ] Confirm treatment of direct-message marketing.

## E. Privacy / tracking

- [ ] Confirm affiliate tracking technology/provider.
- [ ] Confirm cookie/tracking consent requirements by jurisdiction.
- [ ] Update privacy policy and cookie notice.
- [ ] Define retention period for affiliate attribution data.
- [ ] Define affiliate dashboard data access.
- [ ] Confirm affiliates cannot collect AgentOS customer data without separate authorisation/DPA.
- [ ] Define breach/security notification obligations if any affiliate receives authorised data access.

## F. Trademark / brand

- [ ] Approve trademark-use licence language.
- [ ] Approve prohibited domain/social handle rules.
- [ ] Approve paid-search trademark bidding policy.
- [ ] Approve use of logos/creative assets.
- [ ] Define process for removing obsolete brand assets.

## G. Fraud / attribution abuse

- [ ] Prohibit cookie stuffing and forced redirects.
- [ ] Prohibit self-referral unless a specific exception is deliberately created.
- [ ] Prohibit fake accounts / synthetic transactions.
- [ ] Define coupon/cashback/deal-site policy.
- [ ] Define browser-extension/adware policy.
- [ ] Define sub-affiliate/network policy.
- [ ] Define suspicious-traffic review and appeal process.

## H. Contract fairness

Counsel should specifically review for Australian small-business unfair-contract-term risk and analogous mandatory-law concerns in other launch jurisdictions.

Review carefully:
- [ ] unilateral commission changes;
- [ ] retroactive forfeiture;
- [ ] one-sided termination;
- [ ] indefinite payment holds;
- [ ] broad indemnities;
- [ ] liability exclusions;
- [ ] unilateral venue/law clauses;
- [ ] AgentOS-only discretion with no objective standard;
- [ ] rights to change attribution after transactions already occurred.

Prefer proportionate, transparent, prospective rules tied to legitimate business/fraud/compliance needs.

## I. Public-facing documents required before launch

- [ ] Affiliate Program landing page.
- [ ] Affiliate Terms.
- [ ] Commercial Schedule / commission table.
- [ ] Disclosure Guide.
- [ ] Brand Guidelines.
- [ ] Prohibited Promotion / Traffic Policy.
- [ ] Privacy Policy update.
- [ ] Cookie notice/consent update if required.
- [ ] Affiliate FAQ.
- [ ] Compliance contact / reporting process.

## J. Internal controls

- [ ] Affiliate application review checklist.
- [ ] Creator/affiliate risk classification.
- [ ] Approved claims library linked to Marketing Claims Register.
- [ ] Evidence-expiry process for claims used by affiliates.
- [ ] Warning/suspension/termination playbook.
- [ ] Commission dispute log.
- [ ] Fraud/chargeback log.
- [ ] Material contract-change notice process.

## Launch rule

Do not open public affiliate enrolment until sections A–I are materially complete and the final terms have been reviewed by qualified counsel for the intended launch jurisdictions.

# AgentOS Affiliate Program — FAQ, Application & Onboarding

**Status:** DRAFT / OWNER + LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

## Application form

Collect only information needed to evaluate fit and administer the program:

- Full name / business name
- Country
- Email
- Website and primary social/channel URLs
- Main audience/topic
- Approximate audience size / traffic range
- Primary promotion methods
- Whether paid ads are used
- Whether email marketing is used
- Whether coupons/cashback/sub-affiliates are used
- Prior affiliate experience
- Why AgentOS is relevant to the audience
- Agreement to program terms and disclosure requirements

Do not request unnecessary sensitive data at application stage.

## Review criteria

Prefer:
- relevant audience fit;
- useful educational content;
- transparent promotional history;
- genuine product interest;
- reasonable traffic quality;
- willingness to disclose commission clearly.

Reject or hold applications with:
- spammy/low-quality sites;
- misleading claims;
- fake engagement;
- piracy, malware, deceptive downloads or unsafe content;
- coupon/cashback/toolbar traffic where not expressly allowed;
- trademark impersonation;
- suspicious self-referral intent.

## Approval flow

1. Application submitted.
2. Basic channel and compliance review.
3. Accept / reject / request more information.
4. Accepted affiliate receives:
   - affiliate ID/link;
   - current commission schedule;
   - approved product messaging;
   - disclosure guide;
   - prohibited-method summary;
   - brand asset pack;
   - contact/support route.
5. Affiliate confirms acceptance of current terms.
6. Tracking becomes active.

## First-day onboarding checklist

Affiliate should complete:
- read program terms;
- read disclosure guide;
- understand claims register boundaries;
- confirm allowed promotion methods;
- confirm brand/trademark restrictions;
- test affiliate link;
- choose first real-use content angle;
- add disclosure before publishing.

## FAQ

### Who can apply?
Creators, publishers, educators, reviewers, communities and other approved partners whose audiences are relevant to AgentOS.

### Is approval automatic?
No. AgentOS may review audience fit, traffic quality, promotional methods and compliance risk.

### What do affiliates earn?
The final commission schedule will be published once approved. Any draft or private working rate is not binding unless included in the current official program schedule.

### How long does attribution last?
The current working rule is 90 days, subject to the final terms and the attribution technology actually implemented.

### What happens if a customer refunds or chargebacks?
Commission tied to a refunded, reversed, fraudulent or chargeback transaction may be reversed or withheld according to the final terms.

### Can I earn commission on my own purchase?
Self-referrals are not permitted unless a specific exception is expressly documented by the program.

### Can I promote with paid search or social ads?
Only where permitted by the current traffic policy. Brand bidding, misleading display URLs, impersonation and restricted trademark use may be prohibited.

### Can I use coupons or cashback?
Only if expressly approved. Coupon, cashback, loyalty, toolbar and browser-extension models should not be assumed eligible.

### Can I use email?
Only to recipients for whom the required permission/consent exists, with legally required sender identification and unsubscribe functionality.

### Can I make claims about savings?
Only claims supported by current approved evidence. Do not promise that AgentOS will save a specific amount, pay for itself, always choose the cheapest AI, or replace every subscription unless and until evidence supports that exact claim.

### Can I say AgentOS works with every AI?
No. Use the approved provider/model-agnostic wording rather than universal compatibility claims.

### Can I use the AgentOS logo?
Approved affiliates may use current approved assets in accordance with brand rules. Do not alter the logo or create an account/page that appears to be the official AgentOS brand.

### What disclosure should I use?
Baseline:

> Ad / Affiliate: I may earn a commission if you purchase AgentOS through this link, at no extra cost to you.

Adapt it to the channel while preserving the meaning and prominence.

### What if I make a mistake in a post?
Correct it promptly. Materially misleading claims, hidden disclosures or prohibited methods may lead to commission reversal, suspension or termination under the final terms.

### Does commission influence how AgentOS routes AI jobs?
No. Affiliate economics are separate from product routing, governance and assurance.

## Ongoing affiliate management

Recommended cadence:
- onboarding check after first published content;
- monthly claims/compliance spot-check for active affiliates;
- quarterly program update;
- immediate notice for material pricing/terms/claims changes;
- documented warning/suspension/termination path for violations.

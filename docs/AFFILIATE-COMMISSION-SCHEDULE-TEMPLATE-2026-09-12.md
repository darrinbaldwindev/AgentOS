**Status:** RECOMMENDED MODEL / OWNER APPROVAL + LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

This schedule is intended to sit beside the Affiliate Program Terms. It allows commercial rates and payout mechanics to be versioned without rewriting the full agreement, subject to the notice/fairness requirements in the final terms.

## Public wording recommendation

> Earn 25% of qualifying net revenue from customers you refer during their first 12 months with AgentOS.

For current annual plans:

> With annual billing, that normally means 25% of the customer's first annual payment, plus qualifying upgrade revenue where applicable.

Do **not** describe the current annual-plan structure merely as “25% recurring for 12 months” without the annual-billing clarification.

## Current plan examples

### Co-worker — $39/year

25% standard commission = **$9.75** before exclusions, reversals or tax treatment.

### Operator — $99/year

25% standard commission = **$24.75** before exclusions, reversals or tax treatment.

### Co-worker → Operator qualifying upgrade

Nominal annual price difference = **$60**.

25% standard commission on qualifying incremental revenue = **$15.00**.

Total standard commission across an initial $39 purchase plus qualifying $60 upgrade = **$24.75**, matching the commission on an initial $99 Operator purchase.

## Qualifying Net Revenue

Recommended definition:

Amount actually received and retained by AgentOS for an eligible purchase during the commissionable period, excluding where applicable:

- GST/VAT/sales taxes;
- refunds and credits;
- chargebacks/payment reversals;
- fraudulent transactions;
- self-referrals;
- prohibited attribution/cookie stuffing;
- unauthorized discounts;
- excluded third-party or pass-through charges;
- any product/add-on explicitly excluded in the Commission Schedule.

Final legal/accounting wording must be reviewed before publication.

## Upgrade attribution

If an attributed customer buys Co-worker and upgrades to Operator within **90 days of the original paid conversion**, the same affiliate should receive commission on the qualifying incremental net upgrade revenue, provided:

- the original transaction was valid;
- attribution remains valid;
- no prohibited activity occurred;
- the upgrade transaction is not refunded/reversed.

Upgrades after this 90-day upgrade window are not commissionable at launch unless a future schedule says otherwise.

## Renewals

**No commission on annual renewals after the customer's first 12 months at launch.**

This keeps customer-acquisition cost bounded until AgentOS has real retention/LTV evidence. The rule may be revisited prospectively after commercial data exists.

## High-performer tier

Recommended invited rate: **30% of Qualifying Net Revenue during the customer's first 12 months.**

Eligibility should be based on a combination of:

- compliant promotion;
- qualified sales volume;
- refund/chargeback quality;
- customer fit;
- low misleading-claim/support burden;
- strategic reach/content value.

Do not promise an automatic public sales threshold before the economics are measured.

## Validation and payout

Recommended operating model:

1. transaction recorded;
2. commission remains pending during a 30-day validation period;
3. refunds, chargebacks, fraud and attribution disputes are reconciled;
4. validated balance becomes payable;
5. payable balance is included in the next monthly payout cycle once any threshold/payment requirements are met.

Suggested launch threshold: **AUD $50 or equivalent**, pending owner/payment-provider approval.

## Refund and reversal mechanics

- full refund → associated commission reversed;
- partial refund → commission reduced proportionally;
- chargeback/fraud → associated commission reversed;
- duplicate/invalid transaction → only legitimate retained revenue counts;
- previously paid reversals may be offset against future commissions where legally and contractually permitted.

## Product scope

### Commissionable by default

- AgentOS Co-worker subscription;
- AgentOS Operator subscription.

### Not commissionable by default

- pass-through AI/API/model-provider charges;
- taxes;
- third-party products/services;
- payment fees charged directly to the customer by another provider;
- products/add-ons not specifically included in the active schedule.

Future first-party add-ons should be individually classified.

## Discount policy

No standing affiliate customer discount is recommended at launch.

The affiliate proposition should initially compete on useful explanation, demonstration and trust rather than coupon-code arbitrage. Any campaign-specific customer discount must be separately approved and budgeted.

## Fair change-control rule

Any public commission schedule should include:

- effective date and version;
- rate and revenue basis;
- attribution rule;
- commissionable period;
- payout timing;
- reversal conditions;
- excluded revenue/products;
- change-notice rule;
- link/reference to the governing Affiliate Program Terms.

Avoid retroactive rate reductions on already-earned, validated commissions except for fraud, error or expressly disclosed reversal conditions.

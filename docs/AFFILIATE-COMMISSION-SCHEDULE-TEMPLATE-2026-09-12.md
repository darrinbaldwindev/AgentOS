## Recommended design principles

1. Keep the schedule simple enough that a creator can estimate earnings without a spreadsheet.
2. Reward genuine new customer acquisition, not attribution gaming.
3. Preserve attribution on a legitimate upgrade if that is commercially approved.
4. Use a validation period long enough to account for refunds/chargebacks without creating unreasonable payment delay.
5. Avoid retroactive rate reductions on already-earned, validated commissions except for fraud, error or reversal conditions expressly covered by the terms.
6. Clearly state whether commission is calculated on gross price, net revenue, tax-exclusive revenue, discounts, refunds and payment fees.
7. Keep bespoke creator rates documented separately and confidential if needed; do not imply every affiliate receives the same negotiated rate.

## Suggested rate scenarios for owner decision

These are decision scenarios, not approved rates.

### Scenario A — Simple one-time
- One-time percentage on first qualifying purchase.
- Easiest to administer and explain.
- Lower long-term liability.
- Less attractive to high-quality SaaS/AI creators.

### Scenario B — 12-month recurring
- Percentage of qualifying subscription revenue for up to 12 months.
- Strong creator proposition.
- Better aligns affiliate reward with retained customer value.
- Requires robust subscription attribution and reversal handling.

### Scenario C — Hybrid
- Higher one-time bounty on initial paid purchase plus a smaller time-limited recurring percentage.
- Strong acquisition incentive without indefinite liability.
- More complex to explain and administer.

## Existing working commercial hypothesis

Prior marketing analysis considered **25% recurring commission for 12 months with a 90-day attribution window**, with upgrades retaining attribution and potential bespoke rates for high performers.

This remains a **HYPOTHESIS / RECOMMENDATION**, not an approved public promise or contractual rate.

## Example calculation language — only after rate approval

> Commission is calculated on qualifying net subscription revenue actually received by AgentOS, excluding taxes, refunds, chargebacks, credits and transactions determined to be fraudulent or ineligible under the Program Terms.

Final legal review should confirm the exact revenue base and whether local tax rules require different treatment.

## Change-control rule

Any public commission schedule must include:
- effective date;
- version number;
- rate/basis;
- attribution rule;
- payout timing;
- reversal conditions;
- change-notice rule;
- link/reference to the governing Affiliate Program Terms.

## Purpose

Define the commercial boundary between Free, $29/year and $99/year without turning paid plans into different AgentOS products.

## Core rule

**Paid tiers contain the same complete AgentOS.** The $29/year and $99/year plans do not remove or add core AgentOS capabilities. Their differentiation is the amount, breadth and quality of AI intelligence/resources available to power AgentOS.

**Free is a deliberately limited taste of AgentOS.** It must be useful enough to establish the product's value and habit, while selected capabilities, capacity and intelligence remain limited so the 30-day upgrade journey has a clear and legitimate value proposition.

## Access model

| Area | Free | $29/year | $99/year |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AgentOS core | Limited taste | Full | Full |
| Basic interface | Taste | Full | Full |
| Essentials interface | Selected experience | Full | Full |
| Tech Head interface | Selected experience | Full | Full |
| Autonomy | Selected modes/limits | Full governed capability | Full governed capability |
| 5-minute heartbeat | Limited/selected | Full | Full |
| 30-minute autonomous mode | Limited/selected | Full | Full |
| Overseer / Project Overseer / worker orchestration | Selected experience | Full | Full |
| Green / assurance experience | Selected experience | Full | Full |
| Advanced controls | Selected/withheld | Full | Full |
| AI model resources | Free/limited | More | Much more |
| AI capacity | Limited | Higher | Highest |
| Model/provider choice | Limited | Expanded | Broadest |

The exact Free feature set and numerical AI limits remain product decisions and should be validated through the 30-day activation/upgrade funnel rather than hard-coded prematurely.

## Three interfaces

The interfaces are views of the same AgentOS, not subscription products:

- **Basic:** simple everyday experience.
- **Essentials:** greater visibility and control for active users.
- **Tech Head:** deep operational, governance and technical controls.

Free users can be shown selected parts of each interface. Paid users receive the complete system regardless of whether they choose Basic, Essentials or Tech Head.

## AI resource preference

Model/resource policy is independent from AgentOS capability access. The product should support policies such as:

- Free/limited models only.
- Free first; use paid intelligence when an authorised trigger and budget permit it.
- Best available within the user's budget/provider policy.

AgentOS should record material model escalation decisions and costs.

## 30-day Free journey

The Free experience should progressively demonstrate value rather than simply display a paywall. Willow, Isla, Jack and Henry can introduce relevant capabilities during the first 30 days, showing what each capability does and what becomes available after upgrade.

The upgrade proposition is therefore:

> **Unlock the whole AgentOS and give it more intelligence.**

The $29/year and $99/year plans should never be positioned as a cut-down versus full AgentOS distinction. Both are full AgentOS; $99 provides substantially greater AI resources/capacity rather than a different control plane.

## Governance invariant

Commercial access controls must never weaken AgentOS safety, authority, verification, Green, PRS, audit, budget or fail-closed governance. Subscription level changes available resources and product access policy; it does not bypass safety controls.

## Relationship to autonomy

Autonomy level, operating cadence and AI resource policy are separate dimensions. A user may, for example, select a 30-minute autonomous mode while choosing free/limited AI only, or permit paid AI escalation within a defined budget.

The 5-minute heartbeat remains a scheduler/control-plane mechanism; the 30-minute mode is an optional operating pattern built on top of governed state and does not itself grant authority.

## Product principle

**Build one AgentOS. Offer a limited Free taste. Make both paid plans complete. Differentiate paid plans primarily through intelligence, model resources, capacity and choice — not by crippling AgentOS.**

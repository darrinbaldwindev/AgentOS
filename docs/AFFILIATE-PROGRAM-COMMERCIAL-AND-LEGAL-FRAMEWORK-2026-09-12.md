# AgentOS Affiliate Program — Commercial & Legal Framework

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Owner:** Marketing Overseer  
**Status:** DRAFT FOR OWNER + LEGAL REVIEW  
**Purpose:** Define the commercial, marketing and legal-control framework for the AgentOS affiliate/creator program before public launch.

> This document is operational/commercial drafting, not legal advice. Final launch terms should be reviewed by qualified counsel in the launch jurisdictions.

## 1. Program distinction

AgentOS should maintain two separate programs:

1. **Creator / Affiliate Program** — performance-based commission for approved promotional activity.
2. **Member Referral Program** — customer/member rewards for referring friends or peers.

Do not mix these in one agreement. They create different expectations, risks, reward structures and disclosure obligations.

## 3. Core legal/compliance principles

### Truthful claims

Affiliates must not make false or misleading claims about AgentOS, including performance, savings, compatibility, autonomy, security, pricing, provider support or future features.

Marketing copy should remain within the current AgentOS Marketing Claims Register and Proof-to-Ad Mapping.

### Clear affiliate disclosure

Affiliates must clearly and conspicuously disclose that they may earn commission from qualifying referrals. The disclosure must be placed with the promotional content, not buried in a profile, footer or remote disclosure page.

Recommended baseline disclosure:

> **Ad / Affiliate:** I may earn a commission if you buy AgentOS through this link, at no extra cost to you.

For short-form social:

> **Ad — affiliate link. I may earn a commission.**

For video:
- disclose verbally near the start where reasonably practical;
- include visible on-screen disclosure;
- repeat in description where applicable.

### Email/SMS/direct-message compliance

Affiliates must not send unsolicited commercial email, SMS or direct messages in violation of applicable law. Purchased lists, scraped contact data, harvested addresses and automated cold-message spam are prohibited.

### Privacy and tracking

Affiliate tracking, cookies and similar technologies must comply with applicable privacy/ePrivacy rules. AgentOS must publish appropriate privacy/cookie notices and obtain consent where required.

Affiliates must not collect customer personal information on AgentOS's behalf unless explicitly authorised under a separate data-processing arrangement.

### Fair contract structure

The affiliate agreement should avoid one-sided terms that could be considered unfair in small-business standard-form contracts, particularly unilateral changes, retroactive forfeiture, arbitrary termination without legitimate basis, indefinite withholding and broad liability provisions unrelated to actual risk.

## 4. Prohibited promotional methods

Unless expressly approved in writing, affiliates must not:

- bid on AgentOS trademarks or confusing variants in paid search;
- impersonate AgentOS or create misleading official-looking accounts/domains;
- use false countdowns, fake scarcity or fabricated discounts;
- claim guaranteed savings, guaranteed performance or guaranteed earnings;
- misrepresent future roadmap items as shipped features;
- use cookie stuffing, forced clicks or invisible redirects;
- overwrite another affiliate's attribution through deceptive mechanisms;
- self-refer for commission;
- purchase through their own affiliate link to manufacture commission;
- spam email/SMS/DM recipients;
- use scraped, bought or harvested marketing lists without lawful basis and explicit program approval;
- run misleading comparison pages or fake reviews;
- suppress material limitations or fabricate testimonials;
- distribute malware/adware/browser extensions for attribution without explicit written approval;
- use incentivised traffic, cashback, coupon, loyalty or deal-site promotion unless specifically approved for that affiliate class;
- create domains or social handles confusingly similar to official AgentOS properties.

## 5. Traffic classes

### Generally permitted with disclosure
- original creator content;
- tutorials/reviews;
- newsletters sent lawfully to opted-in subscribers;
- websites/blogs;
- social media;
- podcast/video descriptions;
- approved comparison content;
- creator communities where promotion is allowed.

### Approval required
- paid search;
- retargeting;
- coupon/deal sites;
- cashback/loyalty;
- sub-affiliate networks;
- browser extensions;
- media buying using AgentOS marks;
- co-branded landing pages;
- bulk email beyond the affiliate's own opted-in list.

## 6. Attribution and commission controls

The final terms should define:

- attribution window and event;
- first-click vs last-click or other model;
- treatment of direct return visits;
- treatment of existing customers;
- treatment of upgrades/downgrades;
- refunds, reversals and chargebacks;
- taxes, VAT/GST withholding where applicable;
- minimum payout threshold, if any;
- payment schedule;
- currency and payment-provider fees;
- fraud review/hold periods;
- disputed attribution process;
- dormant account handling;
- termination effects on accrued vs future commissions.

No commission should become payable on a transaction that is refunded, fraudulent, charged back or otherwise non-qualifying under the published terms.

## 7. Monitoring and enforcement

AgentOS should maintain a reasonable affiliate compliance program:

1. onboarding education;
2. mandatory disclosure examples;
3. approved-claims guidance;
4. periodic sampling of affiliate content;
5. documented warnings/corrections;
6. suspension for material violations;
7. termination for fraud, repeated deception, spam or unlawful promotion;
8. preserved evidence of enforcement decisions.

## 8. Jurisdiction notes

### Australia
- Australian Consumer Law applies to social media promotions and paid/incentivised influencer content.
- Claims must be truthful, accurate and supportable.
- Commercial electronic messages generally require consent, sender identification and a functional unsubscribe mechanism.
- Australian unfair-contract-terms rules can apply to standard-form small-business contracts; one-sided change/termination/penalty clauses require care.

### United States
- FTC endorsement rules require clear and conspicuous disclosure of material connections.
- Affiliate relationships should be disclosed close to the endorsement/link.
- Advertisers may have responsibility for training/monitoring affiliates they pay or direct.
- CAN-SPAM requirements apply to commercial email, including accurate headers/subjects, ad identification, a valid postal address and opt-out mechanism.

### United Kingdom
- Affiliate content is advertising under the CAP framework when commission is earned from clicks/sales.
- Advertising must be obviously identifiable before or at the point consumers engage with it.
- PECR/UK GDPR requirements apply to electronic direct marketing and tracking technologies where relevant.

# AgentOS Advertising Sets — Free AI + Operator — 2026-09-12

**Status:** MARKETING DRAFT / VALIDATION REQUIRED
**Use:** Meta, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn and Content360 repurposing.

## Campaign A — Make More of Free AI

### Objective
Acquire attention from people who already use AI but dislike subscription sprawl or feel they are not getting enough value from free tools.

### Ad A1 — Core hook

**Primary text**
You probably already have access to more AI than you use effectively.

Free AI. Local AI. Existing subscriptions. Specialist tools.

AgentOS is designed to help coordinate the AI available to you instead of assuming every job needs another premium subscription.

**Headline:** Make More of Free AI
**Description:** Use what you already have.
**CTA:** Learn More

### Ad A2 — Subscription fatigue

**Primary text**
Before you pay for another AI subscription, make better use of the AI you already have.

AgentOS is designed to coordinate free, local and paid AI according to the job, your preferences and your limits.

**Headline:** Stop Collecting AI Subscriptions
**Description:** Put the AI you already have to work.
**CTA:** See How It Works

### Ad A3 — Free-first

**Primary text**
A stronger AI model is not necessary for every task.

AgentOS is designed to use included/free capability where it is suitable and bring in stronger paid capability according to your policy and limits.

**Headline:** Free AI First
**Description:** Spend when the job justifies it.
**CTA:** Learn More

### Ad A4 — Challenge

**Primary text**
How much useful work can AgentOS get done using free AI?

We're building a Free-AI Effectiveness Benchmark around real jobs: research, planning, summaries, comparisons, reports and multi-step tasks.

Follow the evidence, not the hype.

**Headline:** The Free AI Challenge
**Description:** Real jobs. Measured results.
**CTA:** Follow the Test

**Gate:** Do not publish measured-result language until benchmark execution exists. Before then use “we plan to test”.

## Claim rules

Do not use:

- “AgentOS will save you $X.”
- “Pays for itself.”
- “Replace all your AI subscriptions.”
- “Always chooses the cheapest/best model.”
- “Works with every AI.”
- “Night Shift works while every computer is powered off.”

Permitted current marketing direction:

- “designed to save time”;
- “designed to reduce unnecessary AI spend”;
- “make the most of free, local and paid AI”;
- “$99/year — less than $2/week”;
- “use the AI you already have”;
- “AgentOS helps coordinate the work.”

## Testing priorities

1. Free-AI value vs Stop Managing AI.
2. $99 less-than-$2/week vs time-value framing.
3. Character-led trust vs feature-led coordination.
4. General page vs audience-specific landing page.

Do not optimise solely for click-through. Compare downstream plan-page interest and meaningful activation where measurable.

# AgentOS Affiliate Program Terms — Draft v0.1

**Status:** DRAFT FOR OWNER + LEGAL REVIEW — NOT FOR PUBLICATION OR ACCEPTANCE YET

These draft terms are intended as a working commercial/legal structure for counsel review. They are not final legal terms.

## 1. Program

The AgentOS Affiliate Program allows approved participants to promote AgentOS using authorised referral links or codes and to earn commission on qualifying transactions under these terms.

Participation is by approval only. AgentOS may maintain separate rules or commercial schedules for different affiliate classes, provided those differences are clearly disclosed to the affected affiliate and do not retroactively reduce accrued qualifying commission.

## 2. Eligibility and application

Applicants must provide accurate information and have legal capacity to enter into this agreement. AgentOS may reject applications based on brand-safety, compliance, fraud, traffic-quality or market-fit considerations.

Affiliates are independent businesses/creators and are not employees, agents, franchisees, partners or representatives authorised to bind AgentOS.

## 3. Approved promotion

Affiliates may promote AgentOS through approved websites, social media, video, newsletters, podcasts, communities and other channels consistent with these terms and applicable platform rules.

Affiliates must:
- describe AgentOS truthfully;
- keep pricing and feature claims current;
- use required advertising/affiliate disclosures;
- comply with applicable advertising, privacy, spam and consumer-protection laws;
- correct materially inaccurate content promptly after notice.

## 4. Required disclosure

Every endorsement or promotion that may generate affiliate compensation must clearly disclose the commercial relationship in a way consumers are likely to notice and understand before or at the time they act on the recommendation.

Suggested disclosure:

> Ad / Affiliate: I may earn a commission if you purchase AgentOS through this link, at no extra cost to you.

Platform disclosure tools may be used where appropriate, but affiliates remain responsible for making the commercial relationship sufficiently clear under applicable law.

## 5. Prohibited conduct

Affiliates must not:
- make false, misleading or unsubstantiated claims;
- claim to be AgentOS or an official AgentOS representative;
- use AgentOS trademarks in confusing domains, usernames or account names;
- bid on restricted AgentOS brand terms in paid search without written permission;
- use fake reviews, fabricated testimonials or undisclosed incentives;
- use spam, scraped lists, harvested contact details or unlawful cold outreach;
- use cookie stuffing, forced clicks, invisible redirects or attribution hijacking;
- place affiliate links in malware, adware or unapproved browser extensions;
- create fake scarcity, fake discounts or false countdown timers;
- self-refer or manufacture transactions primarily to generate commission;
- use coupon, cashback, loyalty, incentive or sub-affiliate traffic without prior written approval;
- promote prohibited, illegal, hateful, deceptive or unsafe material in connection with AgentOS.

## 6. Attribution

The intended attribution window is **90 days**, subject to final technical specification and legal review.

Before launch, the final terms must specify:
- attribution event;
- first-click/last-click or other attribution model;
- treatment of cookies/device changes;
- treatment of existing customers;
- treatment of upgrades and later purchases;
- direct-return attribution;
- cross-device limitations;
- exclusions for non-qualifying traffic.

Where tracking technology cannot reliably attribute a sale, AgentOS is not required to infer attribution from unsupported claims, but should provide a reasonable dispute process for affiliates who present credible evidence.

Commission is earned only on qualifying transactions that remain valid after applicable refund, fraud and chargeback review.

AgentOS should not retroactively reduce commission already validly earned under the published schedule, except where the underlying transaction is refunded, fraudulent, charged back, duplicated or otherwise non-qualifying under terms that applied when it occurred.

## 8. Refunds, chargebacks and reversals

Commission may be reversed for:
- refunded purchases;
- payment chargebacks;
- fraudulent or unauthorised transactions;
- duplicate orders;
- transactions generated through prohibited methods;
- self-referrals where prohibited.

Any hold/review period should be stated in the commercial schedule.

## 9. Payments and tax

Before launch, AgentOS must publish:
- payment cadence;
- minimum payout threshold, if any;
- supported payout methods;
- payout currency;
- responsibility for payment-processing fees;
- required tax information/forms;
- treatment of GST/VAT/sales-tax documentation where applicable.

Affiliates are responsible for their own taxes and business obligations except where AgentOS is legally required to withhold or report amounts.

## 10. Intellectual property

AgentOS grants approved affiliates a limited, revocable, non-exclusive licence to use approved AgentOS trademarks and marketing assets solely for compliant promotion of AgentOS during participation in the program.

Affiliates must follow current brand guidelines and must not modify marks in ways that create confusion, imply ownership, or suggest an endorsement beyond participation in the affiliate program.

## 11. Content and claims

Affiliates may express genuine opinions and reviews. They must distinguish opinion from factual product claims and must not present roadmap items, beta functions or unverified capabilities as generally available.

Where AgentOS provides approved claims or evidence, affiliates may use them accurately and in context. AgentOS may require correction or removal of obsolete factual claims when product conditions change.

## 12. Privacy and customer data

Affiliates must not collect or process customer personal information on behalf of AgentOS unless AgentOS has expressly authorised the activity and appropriate privacy/data-processing terms are in place.

Affiliates are responsible for privacy compliance on their own sites, lists and marketing channels.

## 13. Email, SMS and direct messaging

Affiliates may use their own lawfully obtained, properly consented marketing lists where permitted by applicable law and platform rules.

Purchased, scraped or harvested lists are prohibited. Affiliates must honour unsubscribe/opt-out requests and identify commercial messages as required by applicable law.

## 14. Monitoring and cooperation

AgentOS may reasonably monitor publicly available affiliate promotions for compliance and may request copies/screenshots of promotions or traffic information where necessary to investigate a specific compliance or fraud concern.

Affiliates should receive reasonable notice and an opportunity to correct ordinary non-fraud compliance issues where appropriate. Immediate suspension may occur for serious fraud, deception, malware, unlawful spam, impersonation or material customer harm.

## 15. Suspension and termination

Either party may end participation by notice.

AgentOS may suspend or terminate immediately for serious fraud, unlawful conduct, material deception, impersonation, malware, deliberate attribution abuse or repeated material breach after warning.

Termination should not automatically forfeit valid accrued commission unrelated to the breach. The final agreement should specify what happens to pending/refundable transactions and future recurring commission, if recurring commission is adopted.

## 16. Changes to terms

AgentOS may update the program prospectively for legitimate business, legal, technical or fraud-prevention reasons.

Material changes to commission, attribution or eligibility should be notified in advance where reasonably practicable and should apply prospectively rather than silently rewriting already-earned economics.

Affiliates should have the right to stop participating if they do not accept material future changes.

## 17. Warranties and liability

The final legal version should contain proportionate warranties, disclaimers and liability limits appropriate to the contracting entity and governing law.

It should not attempt to exclude rights or liabilities that cannot lawfully be excluded, and should avoid unnecessarily one-sided provisions that may create unfair-contract-term risk.

# AgentOS Marketing Integration + Brand Strategy — 2026-09-13

**Owner:** ChatGPT Portfolio Overseer / Marketing direction reconciliation  
**Status:** CURRENT STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATION / IMPLEMENTATION-EVIDENCE GATED  
**Scope:** Product integration prioritisation, marketing leverage, partner/distribution strategy.  

## Purpose

Capture the current decision that AgentOS integrations should be prioritised not only by technical capability, but also by customer usefulness, brand recognition, trust transfer, distribution potential and realistic integration feasibility.

AgentOS should not connect to products merely to display logos. Every public integration claim must correspond to a real, supported, evidence-backed connection path. Commercial or affiliate status must not influence technical model/worker selection.

## Current commercial context

Current active commercial ladder remains:

> Free -> $39/year Co-worker -> $99/year Operator -> optional add-ons / BYO AI

Operator is the recommended mainstream paid destination and should have the broadest normal-plan integrations, provider/model choice, persistence, scheduling and recovery/monitoring envelope while preserving the same governance and safety invariants as other plans.

## Integration strategy

AgentOS should favour a deliberately curated initial set of approximately 10–15 high-value integrations instead of chasing a large shallow connector count.

Ranking criteria should include:

1. technical capability value;
2. customer usefulness / frequency of use;
3. brand recognition and trust transfer;
4. integration/API/MCP feasibility;
5. distribution or marketplace potential;
6. reliability and maintainability;
7. entitlement/licensing fit;
8. privacy, security and approval requirements;
9. commercial/partner opportunity as a secondary consideration only.

Commercial incentives, affiliate commissions or partner economics must never determine technical worker/model ranking.

## Integration architecture tiers

AgentOS should communicate integrations in three layers:

### 1. Native flagship integrations

Polished direct AgentOS experiences for frequently used, high-recognition services. Likely examples include Google, Microsoft, Meta, GitHub, Shopify, Slack, Canva, Figma, Notion, Salesforce, HubSpot and Stripe.

### 2. Specialist AgentOS workers

External products AgentOS can delegate bounded jobs to under AgentOS authority, policy, receipts and assurance. Examples include Lovable, Base44, Bolt, v0/Vercel, Manus, Intercom Fin, Replit, Cursor and Factory.

### 3. Long-tail integration fabric

MCP, ACP, Zapier, n8n, Make and generic API adapters provide access to the broader ecosystem without requiring AgentOS to maintain a native adapter for every service.

## Brand-leverage principle

AgentOS is a new brand. Integrations with globally recognised products can provide trust shortcuts for customers because those companies have already invested heavily in awareness and credibility.

This creates legitimate marketing value when the integration is real:

- "Connect AgentOS to Google Workspace"
- "Works with Microsoft 365"
- "Integrates with Shopify"
- "Use AgentOS with Slack"
- "Create with Canva and Figma through AgentOS"

However, AgentOS must not imply endorsement, certification, official partnership or co-branding rights unless the relevant vendor relationship actually exists and trademark/API rules permit the wording.

A future marketing presentation may group recognizable verified integrations under a message such as:

> **AgentOS brings the AI and apps you already use into one governed workspace.**

And, once technically supportable:

> **Plus thousands more through MCP and automation connectors.**

These are marketing directions only until the underlying integration evidence exists.

## Claims discipline

Do not publicly claim an integration until:

1. the interface is defined;
2. authentication/entitlement requirements are understood;
3. least-privilege scopes are identified;
4. an AgentOS adapter/worker contract exists;
5. a real safe test task succeeds;
6. evidence and verification are retained;
7. failure/revocation behaviour is defined;
8. relevant trademark/marketplace rules are checked.

Distinguish clearly between:

- candidate;
- implemented;
- tested;
- verified;
- marketplace-listed;
- official partner.

Never collapse those states into one marketing claim.

## Architectural invariant

External products may provide models, workers, tools, automation, applications, storage, communications or distribution. They must not become a second canonical AgentOS scheduler, mission ledger, authority engine, worker registry, memory system, Green system or PRS system.

The AgentOS Overseer remains the governed coordinator. External capabilities remain subordinate to AgentOS authority, policy, receipts, evidence and assurance.

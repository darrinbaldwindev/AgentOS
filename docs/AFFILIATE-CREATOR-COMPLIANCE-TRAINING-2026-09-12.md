# AgentOS Affiliate Creator Compliance Training

**Status:** DRAFT / OWNER + LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

## Purpose

Give affiliates a short, practical compliance guide they can understand before publishing.

This training is not legal advice. It is program guidance. The final Affiliate Program Terms and applicable law control.

## Rule 1 — Say that you may earn money

If you may receive commission, make that commercial relationship clear where the recommendation appears.

Good baseline:

> Ad / Affiliate: I may earn a commission if you purchase AgentOS through this link, at no extra cost to you.

Do not hide the disclosure:
- at the bottom of a long page;
- only in your profile bio;
- behind a “more” expansion where avoidable;
- inside a vague phrase such as “partner link” if the financial relationship is not obvious.

For short-form video, use both visible disclosure and spoken disclosure where practical.

## Rule 2 — Only say what is currently supported

Safe examples:
- “AgentOS is designed to help you make better use of the AI available to you.”
- “AgentOS is provider/model agnostic by design.”
- “Operator is $99/year — less than $2 a week.”
- “AgentOS coordinates planning, execution, permissions and assurance through its built-in agent roles.”

Do not say unless formally verified and approved:
- “AgentOS will save you more than $99 a year.”
- “AgentOS always chooses the cheapest AI.”
- “AgentOS always chooses the best AI.”
- “AgentOS replaces every AI subscription.”
- “AgentOS works with every AI.”
- “AgentOS runs while your computer is off.”
- “AgentOS is fully autonomous.”
- “AgentOS guarantees results.”

## Rule 3 — Demonstrations must be real

If you show a task/result:
- use an actual run or clearly label a mockup/simulation;
- do not fabricate outputs;
- do not imply a prototype feature is generally available;
- do not remove warnings/limitations just to make the result look better.

## Rule 4 — Comparisons must be fair and current

If comparing AgentOS with another product:
- verify current pricing/features before publication;
- distinguish opinion from fact;
- do not invent competitor weaknesses;
- do not claim equivalent capability without evidence;
- include material limitations where omission would mislead.

## Rule 5 — Do not spam

Do not promote AgentOS using:
- purchased or scraped lists;
- unsolicited bulk email or messages;
- automated comment spam;
- mass unsolicited DMs;
- fake communities/accounts;
- misleading browser popups/downloads.

Email/SMS promotion must comply with local permission, identification and unsubscribe requirements.

## Rule 6 — Do not manipulate attribution

Never use:
- cookie stuffing;
- forced clicks;
- invisible redirects;
- attribution hijacking;
- fake transactions;
- self-referral schemes;
- unapproved browser extensions/toolbars;
- misleading coupon pages.

## Rule 7 — Respect the brand

You may identify yourself as an approved AgentOS affiliate where permitted.

Do not:
- pretend to be AgentOS;
- register misleading official-looking accounts/domains;
- alter logos in misleading ways;
- imply endorsement beyond affiliate approval;
- use restricted brand bidding or trademark use contrary to current program policy.

## Rule 8 — Correct mistakes quickly

If AgentOS changes a feature, price, claim or program rule and your content becomes inaccurate:
- correct material inaccuracies promptly;
- remove stale price/feature claims where needed;
- update disclosures if the relationship changes.

## Pre-publish 10-second check

Before posting, ask:
1. Is my affiliate relationship obvious?
2. Is the claim current and supported?
3. Is the demo real or clearly labelled?
4. Is the CTA honest?
5. Would a reasonable viewer understand what I gain if they buy?

If any answer is no, fix it before publishing.

## Escalate before publishing when

Ask the AgentOS affiliate/program contact before using:
- earnings or ROI claims;
- “cheapest/best” claims;
- medical/legal/financial use-case implications;
- sweepstakes/giveaways;
- coupon/cashback incentives;
- paid search on AgentOS trademarks;
- sub-affiliate networks;
- automated email/SMS campaigns;
- claims based on unreleased features.

# AgentOS Marketing Pricing + Referral Validation Brief

**Owner:** Marketing Overseer  
**Date:** 2026-09-07  
**Status:** RECOMMENDATION / HYPOTHESIS / VALIDATION REQUIRED

## Purpose

Validate the revised commercial hypothesis before hard-coding pricing, reminder cadence, referral incentives or tier boundaries.

Current working ladder to test:

- **Free** — limited but useful taste.
- **$39/year** — Co-worker / natural paid bridge.
- **$99/year** — Operator / desired destination for active users.
- **Subscriptions / BYO AI** — additional capability for serious users.

The current $39 price is a challenger to the earlier $29/year hypothesis. It is not approved final pricing.

## Core hypotheses

1. $39/year improves unit economics if users never reach $99 while remaining low-friction enough for mass-market conversion.
2. The narrower $39→$99 gap may make $99 feel more attainable rather than less distinct.
3. Free users should meet Willow, Isla, Jack and Henry within their first week, primarily at task-relevant moments rather than rigid date gates.
4. Days 1–30 should continue contextual value education and upgrade discovery.
5. After Day 30, an approximately seven-day reminder cadence may work only if reminders are behaviour/value-triggered, not repetitive generic sales nags.
6. A member-get-member referral loop can become a durable acquisition channel separate from the creator/affiliate programme.
7. The strongest referral behaviour should occur after users have experienced demonstrable value, not at signup.

## Price test

Test at minimum:

- Control: **$29/year**
- Challenger: **$39/year**
- Destination: **$99/year**

Measure:

- paid conversion rate
- revenue per activated user
- $29/$39→$99 upgrade intent and eventual paid upgrade
- perceived value
- perceived gap between middle and $99 tier
- whether the middle tier feels like a permanent stopping point
- cancellation/refund intent where applicable

Do not choose $39 merely because it produces more revenue per sale. Evaluate total conversion and downstream $99 progression.

## First-week character introduction

All four AgentOS roles should become understandable during the first week, but introductions should be event-driven where possible.

- **Willow** — first meaningful planning moment.
- **Isla** — first meaningful execution moment.
- **Jack** — first permission/risk/authority boundary.
- **Henry** — first assurance/checking moment.

If an active user reaches all four situations on Day 1, allow all four introductions on Day 1. Do not artificially delay capability understanding.

Character education must remain functional and brief. The user should learn what AgentOS is doing, not be forced through persona backstory.

## Day 1–30 nurture

Use the first 30 days as an upgrade-discovery period rather than a calendar-gated feature-unlock period.

Candidate messages should be driven by actual use, for example:

- repeated manual task → show saved workflow / scheduled capability
- recurring task attempt → explain Night Shift / Autonomy Hours
- free-capacity constraint → explain expanded paid capability
- complex job → explain broader capability in $39/$99
- repeated evidence checks → show stronger assurance/recovery options
- multiple connected tools → explain broader integration scope

Avoid generic daily upgrade prompts.

## Post-Day-30 reminder hypothesis

Test an approximately **7-day reminder interval** only when there is a fresh, user-specific reason to upgrade.

Examples:

- "You manually ran this task 3 times. Operator could schedule it."
- "You completed 18 AgentOS jobs this month. Co-worker gives you more capacity."
- "You tried to add a second recurring routine. Operator supports more autonomous work."

Suppression rules should include:

- no reminder if one was recently dismissed
- no reminder without a new value signal
- no repeated identical wording
- allow user to reduce/disable promotional reminders
- never obscure safety, approval or task-completion UI with upgrade messaging

## Referral strategy

Maintain two distinct programmes:

### 1. Creator / Affiliate programme

For publishers, influencers, reviewers and commercial partners. Existing working hypothesis remains separate and requires independent commercial approval/validation.

### 2. Member referral programme

For ordinary AgentOS users who voluntarily refer people they know.

Potential rewards to test:

- extra AI allowance
- extra Night Shift runs
- temporary premium capability
- account credit
- temporary $99/Operator trial capability
- other low-marginal-cost benefits

Do not assume cash is the best consumer referral reward.

## Referral trigger

Do not ask for referrals immediately after signup.

Candidate eligibility triggers:

- X meaningful jobs completed
- first successful multi-step job
- first Morning Brief / Night Shift success
- repeated weekly use
- high satisfaction signal

Candidate referral CTA:

> **Know someone who would find AgentOS useful?**

Desired organic word-of-mouth concept:

> **Instead of figuring out which AI to use, I just give AgentOS the job.**

This is a target user expression, not an official performance claim.

## Referral positioning

AgentOS should aspire to be described by users as a useful/simple way to access and use multiple AI capabilities, but official marketing must not claim "the best way to access AI models" until comparative evidence supports that claim.

Safer working language:

- **A simpler way to access and use AI.**
- **AgentOS brings your AI together and helps you make the most of it.**
- **Put AI to work.**

## Referral economics validation

For each reward structure model:

- CAC equivalent
- incremental activation
- paid conversion
- $39/$99 mix
- referral fraud/abuse risk
- reward liability
- marginal AI/resource cost
- retention of referred vs non-referred users

A referral programme is not successful merely because links are shared; referred users must activate and retain at acceptable economics.

## Recommended initial test sequence

1. **Message test:** $29 vs $39 middle-tier framing.
2. **Pricing intent test:** $29/$39/$99 side-by-side with realistic entitlements.
3. **Behaviour test:** first-week task-triggered Willow/Isla/Jack/Henry education.
4. **Nurture test:** contextual 30-day upgrade prompts vs generic cadence control.
5. **Post-30 test:** weekly value-triggered reminder vs no reminder.
6. **Referral test:** no referral prompt vs post-value referral prompt.
7. **Reward test:** AI allowance vs Night Shift capability vs account credit.
8. **Actual paid validation:** real purchases/referrals once product/evidence permits.

## Evidence boundary

- $39/year is **HYPOTHESIS / TEST CANDIDATE**.
- Seven-day reminder cadence is **HYPOTHESIS**.
- Member referral incentives are **RECOMMENDATION / TEST REQUIRED**.
- No conversion lift, referral rate, CAC improvement or $99-upgrade improvement is currently VERIFIED.
- No production pricing, billing, referral tracking, affiliate attribution or entitlement change is authorised by this document.

## Current recommendation

Proceed with **Free → $39 challenger → $99** as the next commercial test structure, while retaining $29 as the control. Introduce all four AgentOS roles during the first week through real product moments, continue contextual education through Day 30, test value-based reminders after Day 30, and develop member referrals as a separate acquisition loop from creator affiliates.

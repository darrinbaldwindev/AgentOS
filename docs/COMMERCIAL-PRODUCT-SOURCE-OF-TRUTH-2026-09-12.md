# AgentOS Commercial Product Source of Truth — 2026-09-12

**Status:** CURRENT PRODUCT / COMMERCIAL DIRECTION  
**Scope:** Marketing, product packaging, entitlement design, onboarding, pricing, conversion and claim discipline.  
**Supersedes for active pricing:** earlier Free / $29 / $99 commercial pricing references. Historical documents remain valid only for their dated research context.

## Active commercial ladder

### Free
Purpose: useful taste of AgentOS that proves the product idea without feeling broken.

Customer promise:

> **AgentOS helps you.**

Characteristics:
- genuinely useful interactive/on-demand experience;
- Willow, Isla, Jack and Henry all appear in the Free journey;
- limited capacity, persistence, concurrency, integrations and unattended scope;
- free/local AI should be preferred where suitable;
- tightly bounded demonstrations of scheduled/autonomous behaviour may be included when implemented and safe;
- universal trust controls remain available.

### $39/year — Co-worker
Purpose: low-friction paid step for users who are not yet ready for Operator.

Customer promise:

> **AgentOS works with you.**

Characteristics:
- full core AgentOS experience under the same control plane;
- more memory/context, capacity and connected tools than Free;
- repeatable workflows and multi-step delegation;
- limited scheduled work / Night Shift taste when implemented;
- moderate concurrency and queue depth;
- broader AI/provider choice and more resource allowance;
- designed as a stepping stone, not the preferred destination.

### $99/year — Operator — RECOMMENDED
Purpose: mainstream destination for users who want AgentOS to work for them regularly.

Customer promise:

> **AgentOS can work for you, on your schedule.**

Primary commercial message:

> **$99 a year — less than $2 a week.**

Characteristics:
- everything in Co-worker;
- broader autonomy envelope, subject to user authority and policy;
- substantially more scheduled and recurring work;
- fuller Night Shift and Morning Brief experience when implemented;
- multiple autonomy windows where supported;
- higher concurrency, larger queues and greater persistence;
- broader integrations and more advanced recovery/routing/monitoring;
- highest normal-plan AI/resource allowance and widest provider/model choice;
- intended recommended/default paid tier for serious regular use.

### Optional add-ons / BYO AI / specialist capability
For larger quotas, specialist providers, business/team capability, premium services, advanced autonomy entitlements or provider costs not economically bundled in the base tiers.

## One AgentOS — different entitlement envelopes

Commercial plans do **not** create separate AgentOS products or separate governance systems.

The invariant is:

> **Same AgentOS core -> different entitlement envelope -> same governance invariants.**

Plans may differ by:
- capacity;
- persistence;
- autonomy windows;
- schedule breadth;
- recurring work;
- concurrency;
- queue depth;
- integrations;
- routing/provider choice;
- recovery/monitoring depth;
- resource allowance.

Plans must **not** differ by weakening or removing core trust/safety controls.

## Universal trust controls

These must not be paywalled or weakened by tier:
- pause / stop / kill controls;
- authority and approval boundaries;
- privacy/data controls;
- Jack safety/governance checks;
- basic evidence and Henry assurance;
- revocation;
- basic history/export where appropriate;
- fail-closed behaviour.

## Free AI / local AI value proposition

A core AgentOS marketing principle is:

> **AgentOS helps you get more from the AI you already have — including free AI.**

Supporting messages:

> **Free AI is more powerful when it works together.**

> **Use free AI. Use local AI. Use the AI you already pay for. AgentOS helps put it all to work together.**

> **Your AI doesn't need to cost more. It needs to work better together.**

Product direction:
- free-only should be a first-class routing policy;
- free-first with paid escalation should be a first-class routing policy;
- local AI should be eligible where suitable and authorised;
- BYO keys/subscriptions should be supported where integrated;
- paid AI should be used when it adds enough value and is authorised by the user's policy/budget.

The stronger claim **"AgentOS can run effectively using free AI alone"** is an active product hypothesis to prove through the Free-AI Effectiveness Benchmark before being promoted as a verified performance claim.

## $99 Operator savings story

Operator should be marketed around two value categories:

### Time saved
- fewer manual model/tool choices;
- multi-step delegation;
- repeatable workflows;
- scheduled/recurring work;
- Night Shift / Morning Brief when implemented;
- broader automation and concurrency.

### Unnecessary AI spend avoided
- prefer free/included resources when suitable;
- use local AI where suitable;
- make better use of subscriptions the user already owns;
- avoid defaulting every task to premium AI;
- escalate to paid capability only under authorised policy/budget.

Safe current wording:

> **AgentOS Operator is designed to save time and reduce unnecessary AI spend.**

> **It only needs to save you a small amount of time or AI spend each week to justify less than $2 a week.**

Do **not** claim a guaranteed dollar saving until measured evidence exists.

## Night Shift packaging

Night Shift is a major Operator proposition, but the capability must remain evidence-gated.

Commercial direction:
- Free: demonstration/taste only where safe and implemented;
- $39 Co-worker: limited Night Shift/scheduled work to demonstrate value;
- $99 Operator: fuller Night Shift, more routines/windows, greater queue depth/concurrency and richer Morning Brief experience;
- advanced Mission Autonomy may require additional entitlement and must not automatically be assumed to belong to $99.

Invariant:

> **WHEN AgentOS may work is not the same as WHAT AgentOS may do.**

Effective execution remains subject to user authority, policy, risk, capability, budget, approval, evidence and assurance.

## Character roles

AgentOS presents one AI team rather than a picker of disconnected chatbots.

- **Willow — Planner:** understands, plans and prioritises.
- **Isla — Executor:** carries out authorised work.
- **Jack — Guardian:** enforces permissions, authority, risk and budget boundaries.
- **Henry — Assurer:** checks evidence, quality and outcome.

These roles are product functions, not premium mascots, and should be visible across the journey.

## Competitive position

AgentOS should not claim to be better than every individual AI tool.

Preferred framing:

> **Other products give you powerful AI tools. AgentOS is designed to coordinate them into one governed working system.**

And:

> **You don't have to replace your favourite AI. AgentOS is designed to help you make better use of it.**

The intended category position is a governed AI operating layer for the everyday computer: personal, professional, creator, developer, family and business use.

## Hybrid / provider architecture implications

Marketing assumes a provider-neutral architecture.

Current product direction:
- local AI via an appropriate local-inference path (Ollama is the current recommended candidate, not yet an implementation claim);
- optional subordinate specialist workers where safe and licensed;
- free cloud/API capacity where terms permit;
- paid/BYO providers when authorised.

No external system may become a second canonical scheduler, mission ledger, authority engine, worker registry, memory system, Green system or PRS system.

Commercial/affiliate relationships must never influence technical worker/model ranking.

## Claims discipline

### Safe current claims / directions
- AgentOS is provider/model agnostic by design.
- AgentOS is designed to make the most of free, local and paid AI.
- $99/year is less than $2/week.
- Operator is designed to save time and reduce unnecessary AI spend.
- free-first/included-AI-first is an active product direction.

### Evidence-gated claims
Do not publish as implemented/verified until evidence exists:
- "AgentOS runs effectively using free AI alone";
- "Night Shift works while your computer is off";
- "AgentOS always chooses the cheapest/best AI";
- "AgentOS replaces all AI subscriptions";
- "AgentOS always saves more than $99/year";
- unrestricted Windows-worker capability;
- remote physical execution;
- broad unattended autonomy;
- bundled Ollama/Libra or other third-party runtime without implementation/licensing evidence.

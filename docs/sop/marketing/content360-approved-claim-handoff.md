# SOP-MKT-001 — Marketing Truth to Content360 Handoff

Status: DRAFT / IMPLEMENTATION-DEPENDENT
Owner: SOP Overseer
Upstream owner: Marketing Overseer
Downstream optimizer: Content360 workstream

## Core rule
Marketing Overseer owns the approved marketing proposition and factual claim set. Content360 may transform approved material for channels/formats but cannot create new commercial truth, legal authority or publication authority.

## Handoff packet
Record: project/operator; destination jurisdiction/audience; campaign/objective; approved claim IDs/text; supporting evidence/date; product/version/SKU where relevant; approved price/offer window; stock/delivery limitations; affiliate/sponsor relationship; required disclosures; prohibited claims; brand/persona constraints; approved destinations/URLs; privacy/consent audience basis; publication authority state; expiry/revalidation trigger.

## Content360 allowed transformation
Subject to evidence and channel policy, Content360 may optimize structure, length, formatting, headline variants, keywords, metadata, channel adaptation and scheduling recommendations while preserving the semantic truth of approved claims/disclosures.

## Content360 must not independently change
- price, discount or offer terms;
- product/service capability or availability;
- stock/freight/delivery promise;
- earnings/rewards/performance guarantee;
- refund/warranty/contract/legal meaning;
- affiliate/sponsorship disclosure;
- privacy/consent basis;
- compliance/certification/security claims;
- approved destination;
- runtime/shipped AgentOS capability;
- publication/send authority.

## Changed meaning
If optimization materially changes meaning, creates an implied comparative/superlative claim, introduces urgency/scarcity, changes audience/jurisdiction or removes/obscures a required disclosure, return it upstream for evidence/reapproval.

## Publication
Draft/optimization completion != publication. Live post/email/ad/listing/campaign activation requires canonical publication authority and destination-specific checks.

## Evidence
Preserve source claim set, transformed version, transformation metadata, reviewer/approval, destination/channel, publication result if authorised and performance data separately. Performance data may inform future claims but does not retroactively validate an unsupported claim.

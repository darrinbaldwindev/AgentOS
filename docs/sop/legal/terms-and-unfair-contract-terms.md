# SOP-LEGAL-004 — Terms, Standard-Form Contracts and Unfair-Term Gate

Status: DRAFT / LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED
Jurisdiction baseline: Australia

## Purpose
Prevent AgentOS or a portfolio worker from treating generated Terms of Use, Terms of Sale, subscription terms, supplier terms, franchise terms or other standard-form contracts as legally approved merely because text exists.

## Core rule
A contract template is not legal approval. Before publication or contractual acceptance, identify the parties, jurisdiction, transaction type, consumer/small-business status, renewal/cancellation/payment/refund terms, liability allocation, dispute process and any mandatory statutory rights.

## Gate
1. Identify the exact contract and affected product/project.
2. Record jurisdiction and intended counterparties.
3. Check whether Australian Consumer Law consumer guarantees or unfair-contract-term protections may apply.
4. Flag terms that create one-sided termination, unilateral variation, automatic renewal, broad indemnity, disproportionate penalties, unilateral price change, liability exclusion, evidence control, or similar imbalance for legal review.
5. Ensure terms do not purport to remove non-excludable consumer rights.
6. Record source, retrieval date and review status.
7. Require explicit publication/acceptance authority.

## Fail closed
UNKNOWN applicability, stale legal evidence, unresolved one-sided terms or absent publication authority => LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED / BLOCKED.

## AgentOS boundary
Workers may research, compare, draft and flag. They may not accept contracts, click acceptance on behalf of the owner, publish terms as approved, waive rights, bind a company, or claim compliance without explicit authority.

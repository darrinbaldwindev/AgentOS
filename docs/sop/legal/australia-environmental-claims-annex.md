# SOP-LEGAL-021 — Australia Environmental and Sustainability Claims Annex

Status: DRAFT / LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED
Jurisdiction: Australia
Evidence refreshed: 2026-09-16

## Current baseline
ACCC guidance on environmental claims emphasizes truthful, accurate, evidence-based claims; important information and conditions should not be hidden; broad or unqualified claims such as `sustainable`, `environmentally friendly` or `eco-friendly` can mislead if the overall impression is unsupported. Visuals/logos can also contribute to the representation.

## Claim packet
Before an Australian-facing environmental claim, record: exact wording and visuals; product/service/business scope; ordinary-consumer interpretation considered; measurable benefit; lifecycle boundary; baseline/comparator; methodology/standard; evidence/source/date; material qualifications/trade-offs; certification/logo authorization; supply-chain dependency; future-target assumptions; reviewer; expiry/revalidation trigger.

## Fail closed
Do not publish a material claim where evidence is missing/stale, the claim depends on hidden conditions, a broad impression exceeds the proven benefit, a certification/logo is unauthorised, a future target is presented as achieved, or Content360/AI has broadened a qualified source claim.

This annex is a claim-control aid, not a determination that a claim complies with the Australian Consumer Law. Current ACCC primary guidance must be rechecked before launch.

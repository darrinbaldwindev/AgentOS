# SOP-LEGAL-018 — Environmental and Sustainability Claims

Status: DRAFT / JURISDICTION REVIEW REQUIRED
Owner: SOP Overseer

## Purpose
Prevent broad or ambiguous environmental claims from being generated from supplier copy, marketplace metadata, AI inference or marketing transformation without claim-specific evidence.

## Claim packet
For each claim record: exact wording; product/service/SKU and lifecycle scope; jurisdiction/audience; measurable basis; methodology/standard; evidence source/date; comparison baseline if comparative; qualifications/limitations; supplier/manufacturer provenance; independent verification/certification if claimed; owner approval; expiry/revalidation trigger.

## Rules
- evidence must support the net impression, not merely one narrow literal interpretation;
- broad terms such as `green`, `eco-friendly`, `sustainable`, `carbon neutral`, `recyclable`, `biodegradable`, `renewable`, `zero emissions` or similar require precise substantiation and qualification appropriate to the claim;
- a logo/certification may be claimed only when current entitlement and scope are verified;
- supplier marketing text is evidence to investigate, not automatic proof;
- omission of material lifecycle, disposal, geography or comparison limitations can make an otherwise factual fragment misleading;
- Marketing/Content360 may shorten approved claims only without broadening their meaning or dropping material qualifications.

## Fail closed
UNKNOWN methodology, unsupported certification, stale supplier evidence, ambiguous comparison baseline or a claim broader than the evidence => HOLD/REWRITE, not publication.

## Portfolio application
GlobalShopCo/eBay/Amazon/MyPrimeDelivery: bind claims to exact SKU and current supplier evidence. Affiliate sites: attribute third-party environmental claims and preserve commercial disclosure. AgentOS: efficiency/local-processing claims must reflect measured current behavior, not architecture intent.

Jurisdiction-specific legal review remains required before public launch.

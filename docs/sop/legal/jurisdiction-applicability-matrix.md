# SOP-LEGAL-011 — Jurisdiction Applicability Matrix

Status: DRAFT / LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED
Owner: SOP Overseer

## Purpose
Prevent a portfolio-wide legal template from being mistaken for jurisdiction-specific compliance.

## Rule
Every public/commercial surface must identify relevant jurisdiction and audience before a legal/compliance control is marked satisfied. Australia is the initial baseline; UK and US obligations are separate annexes, not copied assumptions.

## Required matrix columns
- project/property
- operator/legal entity (UNKNOWN if not established)
- target countries/regions
- actual customer/user locations where material
- activity: software / ecommerce / marketplace / affiliate / marketing / content / franchise / other
- personal-data collection
- marketing communications
- tracking/advertising technology
- consumer sales
- affiliate/sponsored content
- user-generated/review content
- children/minors exposure where relevant
- product-safety exposure
- applicable legal-control SOPs
- mandatory/conditional/recommended/review-required classification
- primary/regulator source
- retrieval/effective date
- evidence owner
- unresolved legal questions
- publication/activation state

## Initial portfolio mapping
AgentOS: AU baseline; global expansion requires jurisdiction review. Privacy, automated decisions, contracts/subscriptions, marketing, connected apps and security are material.
Affiliate Websites AU: AU affiliate disclosure, privacy/tracking, advertising claims and commercial-message controls.
Affiliate Websites UK: UK-specific advertising/affiliate, privacy/ePrivacy/direct-marketing and consumer controls require separate current-source annex.
Affiliate Websites USA: federal plus potentially state-specific privacy/advertising/consumer controls require separate applicability analysis.
GlobalShopCo/eBay/Amazon: ecommerce/marketplace, consumer guarantees, pricing, product safety, privacy/tracking and communications.
MyPrimeDelivery: affiliate/commercial disclosure, eligibility/deal freshness, pricing claims, privacy/tracking.
GhostKitchen: consumer claims, pricing, food/franchise/local regulatory controls require project-specific legal review before operation.
Marketing/Content360: downstream publishing must inherit the destination project's jurisdiction and approved-claim constraints.

## Fail closed
No `COMPLIANT`, `LEGAL APPROVED`, `MANDATORY COMPLETE` or equivalent portfolio claim from a generic template. UNKNOWN operator, jurisdiction, audience or activity can block a legal completion claim.

This matrix coordinates review; it is not legal advice.

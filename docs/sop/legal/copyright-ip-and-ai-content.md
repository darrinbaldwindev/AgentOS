# SOP-LEGAL-014 — Copyright, Intellectual Property and AI-Generated Content

Status: DRAFT / LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED
Owner: SOP Overseer

## Purpose
Govern source material, images, text, code, brands, datasets and AI-generated/transformed content without assuming generation or internet availability creates reuse rights.

## Core rules
- Record provenance for material incorporated into public/commercial outputs.
- Do not treat publicly accessible content as public domain.
- Do not remove attribution/licence notices where required.
- Do not claim ownership, exclusivity, registration or licence rights without evidence.
- AI generation/transformation does not automatically resolve copyright, trademark, publicity/personality, confidentiality or contractual restrictions.
- Do not instruct Content360 or another model to paraphrase protected source material merely to evade rights restrictions.

## Required content record where material
Asset/content ID; creator/source; acquisition method; licence/permission basis; permitted uses; attribution; territory/term; modifications; AI involvement; model/provider if relevant; source inputs; brand/person likeness issues; commercial-use status; evidence date; reviewer.

## Code/open source
Maintain dependency/licence inventory and required notices. Model-generated code still requires normal provenance, security and licence review where it reproduces or depends on third-party material.

## AI claims
Do not represent AI-generated content as a real customer testimonial, documentary photograph, human-authored expert opinion or verified factual evidence unless that representation is independently true and disclosed appropriately.

## Escalate
Legal review for uncertain ownership, substantial source reuse, brand/trademark use, real-person likeness/voice, proprietary/confidential inputs, disputed takedowns, or material commercial licensing.

This SOP deliberately avoids declaring the copyright status of AI outputs categorically; that question is jurisdiction- and fact-dependent.

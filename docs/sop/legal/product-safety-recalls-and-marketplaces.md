# SOP-LEGAL-007 — Product Safety, Recalls and Marketplace Publication

Status: DRAFT / LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED
Jurisdiction baseline: Australia

## Purpose
Prevent product research, dropshipping automation, affiliate content or marketplace tooling from publishing/continuing unsafe, banned, recalled or materially unverified products.

## Scope
GlobalShopCo, Shopify/headless surfaces, eBay/Amazon workstreams, MyPrimeDelivery, affiliate product pages and any AgentOS commerce worker.

## Pre-publication gate
For relevant physical products record:
- exact product/SKU/model and supplier;
- applicable mandatory safety standards/bans where identified;
- recall/search evidence and retrieval date;
- warnings/labels/instructions required by available evidence;
- supplier/product traceability;
- material compliance UNKNOWNs;
- marketplace-specific requirements;
- approval state.

## Recall/safety event
1. stop new automated publication/promotion for the affected exact product where identity is sufficiently established;
2. preserve evidence and affected listings/IDs;
3. escalate for owner/legal/compliance assessment;
4. do not silently substitute another product;
5. record any authorised removal, customer communication, supplier action or regulator step;
6. re-enable only after current evidence and explicit authority support it.

## Marketplace distinction
A marketplace accepting a listing does not prove product-law compliance. A voluntary marketplace safety pledge or platform rule must not be represented as the statutory legal test.

## Automation boundary
Workers may monitor public safety evidence and flag/prepare changes. They may not issue a legal recall, contact regulators/suppliers/customers, destroy stock, admit liability or make production listing changes unless explicitly authorised.

## Fail closed
Exact product identity uncertain + credible safety signal => do not claim the product is verified safe. Escalate the uncertainty.

# SOP-LEGAL-022 — Australia Franchise Applicability Gate

Status: DRAFT / LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED
Portfolio: GhostKitchen / Franchise and any future franchise offering
Evidence refreshed: 2026-09-16

## Current baseline
The ACCC states that Australia's Franchising Code of Conduct is a mandatory industry code. A new code commenced 1 April 2025, with some obligations applying from 1 November 2025. Current ACCC guidance states franchisors must prepare prescribed disclosure material; most franchisors must maintain a franchise disclosure register profile, and new franchisors must join the register at least 14 days before entering franchise agreements.

## Applicability gate
Before describing a portfolio concept as an Australian franchise ready for offer, record: operator/franchisor identity; business model and whether the arrangement is legally a franchise; proposed franchisee class/location; agreement structure; fees/royalties/funds; territory; supply restrictions; IP/licence; capital expenditure; earnings/financial claims; termination/restraint; dispute process; disclosure document status; information statement/code-copy obligations; register status; cooling-off/termination obligations; legal reviewer; current code/source dates.

## GhostKitchen boundary
Concept modelling, template design, unit-economics scenarios and candidate operating systems do not authorise offering or selling a franchise. No franchise fee, royalty, earnings representation, disclosure document, agreement, register entry or prospect contact may be treated as approved merely because a template exists.

## Protected actions
Entering agreements, publishing regulated disclosure/register information, accepting money, contacting prospects as an offer, making earnings claims or representing legal compliance require explicit owner authority and qualified legal review.

This gate coordinates evidence; it does not decide legal applicability or certify compliance.

# SOP-LEGAL-013 — Complaints, Disputes and Remedies

Status: DRAFT / JURISDICTION REVIEW REQUIRED
Owner: SOP Overseer

## Purpose
Create a truthful, evidence-preserving process for customer/user complaints, consumer remedies and business disputes.

## Intake record
Complaint ID; complainant/contact channel; product/service/order where applicable; jurisdiction; issue; requested outcome; evidence supplied; relevant terms/policy version; consumer-guarantee/product-safety/privacy/security flags; owner; timestamps; response/action; escalation; closure basis.

## Rules
1. Acknowledge without admitting facts not established.
2. Preserve relevant evidence and avoid deleting records subject to a dispute/legal hold.
3. Determine whether statutory consumer rights, safety, privacy, security, contract or mandatory industry-code obligations may apply.
4. Do not use store terms to deny non-excludable rights.
5. Give accurate escalation options; do not claim the ACCC resolves individual disputes.
6. Route safety, breach, fraud/security and regulator matters to their dedicated SOPs.
7. Human/legal review is required before litigation threats, settlement terms, regulator filings or material admissions unless a separately approved process authorizes them.

## AgentOS boundary
A worker may classify, assemble evidence and draft a response within authority. It may not autonomously waive rights, settle a material dispute, issue a legal admission, contact a regulator or promise a remedy outside approved policy/authority.

## Closure
Closed means the defined complaint workflow has a recorded outcome; it does not mean the complainant agrees, a legal dispute is resolved, or a regulator/court has determined compliance.

Australian baseline: ACCC guidance directs consumers to contact the business first and notes the ACCC does not resolve individual complaints; other agencies, ombudsmen, tribunals or courts may be relevant.

This SOP is operational guidance, not legal advice.

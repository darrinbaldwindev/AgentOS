# SOP-LEGAL-009 — Pricing, Discounts, Scarcity and Promotions

Status: DRAFT / JURISDICTION REVIEW REQUIRED
Owner: SOP Overseer

## Purpose
Prevent misleading price, saving, scarcity, availability, delivery and promotional representations across AgentOS, GlobalShopCo, marketplaces, affiliate sites, MyPrimeDelivery, GhostKitchen, Marketing and Content360.

## Non-negotiable rule
No worker may invent or amplify a price advantage, former price, percentage saving, scarcity, countdown, stock level, delivery promise, earnings outcome, popularity ranking or limited-time condition without current evidence supporting the exact claim.

## Required claim record
For each material promotion retain: product/service; jurisdiction; exact claim; current selling price; comparison/reference price and basis; source; evidence timestamp; stock/availability basis if claimed; start/end; exclusions; delivery/freight assumptions; approval state; publication surface; revalidation trigger.

## Fail-closed conditions
Do not publish when:
- a strike-through/was price lacks defensible evidence;
- a percentage saving cannot be reproduced;
- freight or mandatory fees make the displayed comparison misleading;
- scarcity/countdown is artificial or automatically resets without truthful basis;
- stock/delivery data is stale or unknown;
- an affiliate feed is treated as authoritative after its freshness window;
- a model inference is the only evidence for a factual promotional claim.

## Automation
Content360, Marketing, scheduled jobs and AI workers may transform approved copy but may not alter factual price, savings, availability, delivery, terms or expiry without fresh evidence and authority.

## Evidence states
VERIFIED FACT / REASONABLE INFERENCE / UNKNOWN plus MANDATORY / CONDITIONALLY MANDATORY / RECOMMENDED CONTROL / LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED.

This SOP does not itself approve a promotion.

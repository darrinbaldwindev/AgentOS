# SOP-LEGAL-012 — Competitions, Giveaways, Gifts and Prizes

Status: DRAFT / JURISDICTION REVIEW REQUIRED
Owner: SOP Overseer

## Purpose
Govern promotional competitions, giveaways, gifts, rebates, prizes and chance-based promotions across portfolio properties.

## Core rules
- Never advertise a gift/prize without a genuine intention and operational ability to provide it as offered.
- State material conditions, eligibility, dates, geographic limits, delivery costs, entry mechanics and prize limitations clearly before participation/purchase.
- Never describe a chance to win as though the entrant will win.
- Do not hide expired offers or material limitations in fine print.
- A competition must not launch until jurisdiction-specific permit/licence, trade-promotion, gambling and age requirements have been checked and classified.

## Required promotion record
Operator; jurisdictions; promotion type; eligibility; start/end; prize/gift; value basis; supply evidence; entry method; purchase requirement; draw/selection method; winner notification; fulfilment deadline; delivery/fees; terms version; privacy/marketing consent separation; permit/licence analysis; approval; evidence date.

## Automation
AI/Content360/Marketing may transform approved promotional copy but cannot invent prize value, scarcity, winners, odds, dates, eligibility or legal approval.

Australian baseline: ACCC guidance treats gifts/prizes/cash-back promotions as subject to consumer-law rules and misleading-conduct controls. State/territory promotion rules require separate applicability review.

This SOP does not constitute a permit, licence or legal approval.

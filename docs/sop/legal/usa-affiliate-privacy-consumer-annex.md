# SOP-LEGAL-019 — United States Affiliate, Privacy and Consumer Annex

Status: DRAFT / LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED / STATE-APPLICABILITY REQUIRED
Owner: SOP Overseer

## Scope
Operational baseline for portfolio surfaces directed to United States consumers. This is not a 50-state compliance determination and cannot be copied into public legal text as certification.

## Federal advertising baseline
FTC truth-in-advertising principles require advertising claims to be truthful, non-deceptive/unfair and evidence-based. Endorsements cannot make claims the marketer could not substantiate directly. Material connections that consumers would not reasonably expect should be disclosed clearly and conspicuously.

For affiliate content, record the relationship/program, commission or other material connection, affected content/link/code, disclosure wording/placement, evidence date and destination. Do not assume `affiliate link`, `buy now`, a profile/footer disclosure or an ambiguous discount code necessarily communicates the financial relationship clearly.

## Reviews/testimonials
Use genuine provenance; do not fabricate reviewers, experiences, ratings or consensus. Evaluate current FTC Endorsement Guides and the Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule for the actual surface and conduct before launch.

## Privacy/security baseline
Create an actual US data-flow inventory: categories, purposes, sensitive data, children/teen data, health data, precise location, sale/share/targeted advertising/profiling, processors/third parties, retention, security, deletion/access/correction/opt-out mechanisms, breach flows and state/user location.

FTC Act privacy/security risk remains relevant, but state comprehensive privacy statutes and sector-specific federal/state laws may add different applicability thresholds, notices, rights and opt-out duties. `US compliant` is prohibited without state/operator/activity analysis.

## High-risk applicability gates
Escalate before launch if processing involves children, health information, financial/credit data, biometric data, precise geolocation, employment/tenant decisions, automated consequential decisions, data brokerage, telemarketing/SMS, subscriptions/negative-option billing, or marketplace high-volume seller obligations.

## State matrix
For every US-facing property maintain: operator/entity; states targeted/known users; applicability threshold analysis; controller/business role; sensitive-data categories; sale/share/targeted-ad opt-outs; universal opt-out mechanism obligations where applicable; consumer request rights; processor contracts; retention/minimisation; children's rules; effective dates; source; legal-review state.

## Fail closed
Unknown state applicability, unsupported consent, stale privacy notice, missing material affiliate disclosure, unsubstantiated advertising claim or unresolved sector rule => HOLD the affected launch/claim, not a generic compliance assertion.

## Evidence baseline
Current FTC business guidance is the federal operational starting point; state attorney-general/legislature and other primary regulator sources must be refreshed for each applicable state before approval.

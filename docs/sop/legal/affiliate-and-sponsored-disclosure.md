# SOP-LEGAL-001 — Affiliate and Sponsored Disclosure

**Status:** LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED / IMPLEMENTATION-DEPENDENT  
**Owner:** SOP Overseer  
**Baseline jurisdiction:** Australia  
**Last researched:** 2026-09-15

## Purpose

Prevent consumers from being misled about commercial relationships behind recommendations, reviews, comparisons, endorsements, links, codes and promotional content.

This SOP does not assert that one disclosure phrase satisfies every platform, format or jurisdiction.

## Trigger

Run this SOP before publication when content includes or may include:

- affiliate links or commission-bearing destinations;
- referral links or codes;
- paid sponsorship;
- free/gifted products or services;
- incentives, rebates or benefits connected to promotion;
- ownership or another material commercial relationship that may not be obvious;
- influencer/creator endorsements;
- AI-generated or AI-transformed promotional content containing any of the above.

If relationship status is UNKNOWN, commercial activation fails closed until resolved.

## Core rule

A consumer must not be left with a misleading impression that a commercial endorsement is independent or unsponsored. The disclosure must be clear enough, prominent enough and close enough to the relevant endorsement/action for the relationship to be understood in context.

A footer-only or buried legal-page disclosure MUST NOT be treated as sufficient evidence for an individual endorsement when the relationship is otherwise unclear.

## Procedure

1. Identify the publisher/entity and target jurisdiction(s).
2. Identify every commercial relationship relevant to the content.
3. Classify relationship: affiliate commission, referral benefit, sponsorship/payment, gift/incentive, ownership, other, none, or UNKNOWN.
4. Verify publisher/program approval separately. Consumer-side referral evidence is not publisher affiliate approval.
5. Determine disclosure surface: article/page, comparison table, product card, CTA, email, social post, video/audio, app/UI or other.
6. Place a plain-language disclosure where a reasonable consumer will encounter it before or with the relevant endorsement/commercial action.
7. Do not rely solely on ambiguous shorthand, a remote terms page, hover state, hidden accordion, colour alone or platform metadata when the commercial relationship would otherwise be unclear.
8. Where a platform provides a paid-partnership/ad disclosure tool, evaluate and use it where applicable, but do not assume the tool cures unclear surrounding content.
9. Preserve disclosure text/version, content version, relationship evidence, destination/program identity, jurisdiction and publication evidence.
10. Re-run disclosure review if relationship, destination, platform, page layout, program terms, jurisdiction or content materially changes.

## Affiliate site controls

For Affiliate-Websites and similar properties:

- editorial verification state and commercial approval state remain independent;
- a program may be `VERIFIED` for consumer facts but `COMMERCIAL BLOCKED` for publisher use;
- WordPress/presentation payloads should not become the authority for raw affiliate destinations;
- resolver/governance controls commercial destination selection;
- disclosure state should travel with or be deterministically derived for commercial presentation state;
- no raw tracking URL should be inserted merely because research found one;
- ranking/recommendation must not secretly become commission-ranked unless that methodology is explicitly authorised, accurately represented and legally reviewed.

## Social / creator controls

Before publishing an endorsement:

- disclose payment, gifts, incentives, referral/discount-code benefit or other material brand relationship where it may affect consumer understanding;
- do not imply personal experience that did not occur;
- do not fabricate testimonials or reviews;
- do not make objective product, earnings, price, availability, health, safety or performance claims without evidence appropriate to the claim;
- retain the evidence pack used for the published claim.

## AI controls

AI may draft or transform promotional content but MUST NOT:

- remove a required commercial disclosure;
- convert a blocked/unknown affiliate relationship into an approved one;
- invent commission, reward, discount, earnings or availability facts;
- imply independence where a commercial relationship exists;
- change approved legal/disclosure meaning without re-review.

## Evidence receipt

Minimum record:

- content ID/version;
- publisher/entity;
- jurisdiction;
- commercial relationship type and evidence;
- program/destination ID where relevant;
- disclosure wording/version;
- placement/surface;
- publication timestamp/version evidence;
- reviewer/status;
- unresolved UNKNOWNs.

## Stop conditions

Do not commercially publish/activate when:

- relationship is UNKNOWN;
- publisher approval is UNKNOWN/blocked where required;
- disclosure is absent or materially obscured;
- commercial destination cannot be tied to canonical program identity;
- claim evidence is stale/conflicting;
- applicable legal review is unresolved.

## Australian evidence basis

ACCC influencer sweep material identifies non-disclosure of payment, gifts and other incentives, promotional/referral codes and unclear ownership relationships as a recurring misleading-advertising concern. Australian Consumer Law misleading/deceptive-conduct and false/misleading-representation controls remain the legal backdrop. Exact legal application depends on the facts and requires legal review where material ambiguity remains.

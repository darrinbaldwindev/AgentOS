# SOP-LEGAL-006 — Reviews, Testimonials and Endorsements

Status: DRAFT / LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED
Jurisdiction baseline: Australia

## Purpose
Prevent fake, manipulated, undisclosed or misleading social proof across AgentOS and portfolio commerce/affiliate/marketing surfaces.

## Mandatory operating rule
Never fabricate a customer, reviewer, rating, testimonial, experience or endorsement. AI-generated marketing copy must not be presented as a real person's experience.

## Procedure
Before publishing a review/testimonial/endorsement:
1. establish provenance and whether the reviewer actually used/experienced the product or service;
2. record any payment, gift, discount, affiliate, employment, family, ownership or other material connection;
3. disclose incentives/connections clearly where relevant;
4. do not condition incentives on positive sentiment;
5. do not suppress or selectively route genuine negative reviews in a way that creates a misleading overall impression;
6. do not materially edit a review while presenting it as the reviewer's unaltered words;
7. preserve source/evidence and publication version;
8. investigate credible reports that a published review is fake or misleading.

## AI rule
Models may summarize genuine review evidence if the transformation remains truthful and attributable. They may not manufacture quotes, star ratings, user identities, usage experiences or consensus.

## Affiliate rule
Commercial relationships between a publisher/reviewer and merchant must remain visible enough that the audience is not given a false impression of independence.

## Fail closed
Unverifiable testimonial provenance, undisclosed material relationship or synthetic experience presented as real => BLOCK PUBLICATION.

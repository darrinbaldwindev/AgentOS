# SOP-LEGAL-016 — Portfolio Launch Compliance Checklist

Status: DRAFT / LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED
Owner: SOP Overseer

## Purpose
Provide a fail-closed pre-publication/activation checklist across AgentOS and portfolio projects. Passing this checklist does not itself constitute legal approval.

## Gate 1 — Operator and jurisdiction
- operator/legal entity identified;
- target/customer jurisdictions identified;
- actual activity classified (software, ecommerce, marketplace, affiliate, marketing, franchise, etc.);
- jurisdiction applicability matrix current;
- unresolved operator/jurisdiction UNKNOWNs escalated.

## Gate 2 — Product/service truth
- shipped/runtime capability separated from PRODUCT DIRECTION;
- pricing, subscription, cancellation and entitlement claims match implementation;
- availability/delivery/freight/stock evidence current;
- savings/scarcity/earnings/performance/compliance claims evidenced;
- testimonials/reviews authentic and relationships disclosed.

## Gate 3 — Privacy/data/tracking
- data-flow inventory current;
- privacy-law applicability assessed;
- Privacy Policy/collection notices reflect actual processing where required;
- cookies/pixels/analytics/adtech inventoried and consent/choice implemented where required;
- retention/deletion rules defined;
- breach-response ownership defined;
- automated-decision disclosure gate assessed where relevant;
- cross-border/providers/connectors recorded.

## Gate 4 — Communications and marketing
- consent/legal basis for commercial messages evidenced where required;
- sender identity/contact/unsubscribe controls implemented;
- affiliate/sponsorship disclosure clear and proximate;
- Content360/Marketing inherits approved claims and destination jurisdiction;
- draft authority separated from send/publish authority.

## Gate 5 — Consumer/commerce
- consumer guarantees/refund/returns/warranty wording reviewed;
- unfair-contract-term gate completed where applicable;
- supplier/importer/marketplace responsibility packet complete;
- product safety/bans/recalls/mandatory standards checked;
- seller/fulfilment/returns responsibility clear;
- no unknown freight/cost converted to free-delivery/margin claim.

## Gate 6 — IP/accessibility/security
- content/images/software rights/licences/provenance recorded;
- AI generation not treated as automatic ownership clearance;
- accessibility tested for claimed scope; no unsupported conformance claim;
- vulnerability/security reporting path exists;
- secret/credential handling mapped;
- open-source notices/license obligations reviewed.

## Gate 7 — Governance/evidence
- exact release/head/version identified;
- required owner approvals current;
- protected actions separately authorised;
- evidence packet retained;
- Green/PRS status stated only for their actual scope;
- legal review items explicitly approved by authorised human/legal reviewer before public legal text is marked approved;
- revalidation triggers defined.

## Fail-closed outcome
Any material `UNKNOWN`, `CONFLICTING`, `LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED`, expired evidence or missing required approval prevents a `LAUNCH-COMPLIANCE-COMPLETE` claim. The safe outcome is HOLD with the exact missing evidence/action.

## Portfolio rule
A pass for one project/jurisdiction/version cannot be inherited by another. Generic templates coordinate work; they do not certify compliance.

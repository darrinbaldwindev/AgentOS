# SOP-LEGAL-003 — Consumer Law: Claims, Refunds, Guarantees and Warranties (Australia)

**Status:** LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED / IMPLEMENTATION-DEPENDENT  
**Owner:** SOP Overseer  
**Jurisdiction:** Australia  
**Last researched:** 2026-09-15

## Purpose

Prevent AgentOS and portfolio commerce/marketing systems from publishing misleading claims or policies that improperly restrict Australian Consumer Law rights.

## Claims gate

Before publishing a material commercial claim, identify the evidence supporting it. This includes claims about:

- price/discount/savings;
- product or service characteristics;
- performance/capability;
- stock/availability;
- delivery/freight/timeframes;
- guarantees/warranties;
- earnings/rewards;
- supplier status;
- environmental/sustainability attributes;
- comparative superiority;
- legal/compliance status.

Classify evidence as `VERIFIED FACT`, `REASONABLE INFERENCE`, `UNKNOWN`, `CONFLICTING`, or another approved evidence state. UNKNOWN or conflicting material facts fail closed for positive claims.

## Consumer guarantees

Where Australian Consumer Law consumer guarantees apply:

- statutory guarantees arise automatically;
- business policy/terms cannot remove them;
- remedies depend on the failure and circumstances;
- do not use blanket `no refunds` wording that purports to exclude statutory rights;
- a voluntary warranty is additional to, not a replacement for, consumer guarantees.

A worker may triage a complaint but MUST NOT invent a legal remedy determination where facts or law are uncertain.

## Refund/returns policy gate

Before publication:

1. distinguish change-of-mind policy from statutory remedies;
2. ensure voluntary restrictions do not purport to remove ACL rights;
3. accurately identify seller/responsible business and support route;
4. reconcile policy with actual fulfilment/refund systems;
5. legal-review ambiguous exclusions, high-value products/services or unusual models.

## Warranties against defects

If the business supplies a warranty against defects that falls within prescribed Australian rules, the warranty document may require prescribed information and mandatory ACL text. Do not paraphrase prescribed mandatory wording and call it compliant. Retrieve current regulator/legal source and obtain legal review before production publication.

## Portfolio controls

### GlobalShopCo / ecommerce

Supplier evidence is not customer-facing claim authority. Unknown freight, stock, landed cost or fulfilment must not be converted into `free delivery`, `in stock`, margin or delivery-time claims.

### Affiliate Websites / MyPrimeDelivery

Third-party merchant claims must remain attributed and evidence-dated. Affiliate status does not authorise alteration of merchant price, availability, Prime eligibility, reward or terms.

### GhostKitchen

Unit-economics estimates are not earnings guarantees. Estimated food cost, marketplace fees, demand or contribution scenarios must retain evidence labels and assumptions.

### AgentOS

Product direction is not shipped capability. Do not advertise runtime enforcement, integrations, tiers, refunds, availability, security certification or compliance as live without current evidence.

## Complaint evidence

Preserve:

- order/service/content identifier;
- consumer issue and requested outcome;
- relevant product/service representations;
- applicable policy version;
- evidence/facts;
- remedy/action and authority;
- timestamps and communications;
- escalation/legal review where needed.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- `No refunds` as a blanket exclusion of ACL rights.
- Calling a voluntary warranty the consumer's only remedy.
- Inventing scarcity, discount, delivery or stock.
- Turning supplier MSRP/RRP into a savings claim without a valid comparison basis.
- Turning a forecast/model into guaranteed earnings.
- Publishing `legally compliant`, `certified`, `safe`, or equivalent material claims without evidence appropriate to the representation.

# SOP-LEGAL-020 — United Kingdom Affiliate Advertising Annex

Status: DRAFT / LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED
Owner: SOP Overseer

## Scope
Adds current ASA/CAP advertising controls to the UK privacy/direct-marketing annex. It does not certify any site or campaign.

## Current operational baseline
ASA/CAP guidance treats affiliate marketing as advertising where commission is earned from generated interest/sales. Affiliate content must be obviously identifiable as advertising; the commercial relationship and the marketing nature of the content must not be hidden by layout, platform conventions or ambiguous labels.

## Affiliate publication packet
Record: publisher/site/account; advertiser/merchant; affiliate network/program; product/service; link/code; commission/material connection; exact content; disclosure wording and placement; audience/jurisdiction; substantiation for claims; landing destination; publication authority; evidence/retrieval date; revalidation trigger.

## Rules
- make the advertising nature clear before/at the point users engage with the affiliate recommendation where required by the surface;
- do not rely on a remote footer/profile or ambiguous shorthand when the commercial nature would otherwise be unclear;
- content referring to products tied to affiliate links/codes must be assessed as advertising, not automatically as independent editorial;
- advertiser/brand responsibility may coexist with affiliate responsibility; do not imply the affiliate alone carries all compliance responsibility;
- product, price, availability, savings and environmental claims require their own current substantiation;
- Content360 transformations cannot remove disclosure or make editorial independence claims inconsistent with the commercial relationship.

## Fail closed
Unknown affiliate relationship, unclear ad identification, stale claim evidence, unresolved UK privacy/direct-marketing controls or missing publication authority => HOLD.

## Evidence baseline
Current ASA/CAP advice on recognising ads/social media and affiliate marketing must be revalidated before public launch because guidance and platform presentation evolve.

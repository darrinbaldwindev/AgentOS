# SOP-AFF-002 — Affiliate Program Intake and Approval

Status: DRAFT / IMPLEMENTATION-DEPENDENT

## Separation rule
Consumer eligibility/reputation, publisher affiliate-program availability, publisher application/approval, tracking destination validity and commercial publication are separate facts. Never infer one from another.

## Program record
Program/operator identity; target country; consumer proposition; consumer eligibility; publisher program/network; publisher terms/source; application state; approval evidence; commission basis; cookie/attribution window; prohibited traffic/content; brand/keyword restrictions; tracking/destination pattern; disclosure requirements; payment threshold/method; data-feed/API availability; legal/regulatory category; evidence date; revalidation trigger.

## States
RESEARCHED -> CANDIDATE -> APPLICATION REQUIRED -> APPLICATION SUBMITTED (only with evidence) -> PUBLISHER APPROVED -> DESTINATION VERIFIED -> PUBLICATION APPROVAL REQUIRED -> ACTIVE/PAUSED/REVOKED.

Do not fabricate intermediate states. A consumer referral link is not evidence of publisher affiliate approval.

## Automation boundary
Workers may research, compare and prepare applications. Account creation, application submission, acceptance of network/program terms, credential installation and live affiliate publication are protected unless specifically authorised.

Tracking URLs/credentials must remain out of public logs and unnecessary receipts. Destination changes, approval expiry, program closure or material term changes trigger revalidation.

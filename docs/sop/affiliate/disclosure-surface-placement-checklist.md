# Affiliate Disclosure Surface-Placement Checklist

**Document ID:** SOP-AFF-003
**Status:** DRAFT / JURISDICTION REVIEW REQUIRED

## Principle
Disclosure must travel with the commercial content closely enough that a reasonable user encounters the material relationship before or with the relevant recommendation/action. A generic footer or remote policy page is not assumed sufficient.

## Surface checklist
For each WordPress/headless page, comparison, deal card, CTA, social post, email, video/script or other placement record:
- country/audience and applicable annex;
- affiliate/program relationship and current publisher approval;
- exact destination/resolver identity;
- disclosure text/version;
- placement relative to recommendation/CTA;
- mobile/responsive visibility;
- repeated/embedded content behavior;
- accessibility/readability;
- whether platform-native disclosure tools are also required;
- screenshot/render or equivalent evidence and date.

## Fail closed
Do not publish an affiliate CTA when the relationship, destination, publisher state, required disclosure or material placement evidence is UNKNOWN/conflicting.

Consumer referral availability remains distinct from publisher affiliate approval.

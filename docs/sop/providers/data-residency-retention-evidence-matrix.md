# Provider Data Residency and Retention Evidence Matrix

**Document ID:** SOP-PROVIDER-003
**Status:** DRAFT / IMPLEMENTATION-DEPENDENT / JURISDICTION REVIEW REQUIRED

## Purpose
Prevent model/provider routing from relying on assumed data location, retention or training behavior.

For each provider/service/model path record evidence for: account/product tier, processing locations/residency controls, retention/defaults, zero-retention eligibility if any, training/improvement use, subprocessors where material, deletion controls, encryption claims, enterprise/consumer differences, connector/API differences, evidence source/date and unresolved UNKNOWNs.

## Routing
Combine this matrix with AgentOS data classification and mission authority. A provider being technically available does not make it eligible for a classified payload.

## Claims
Do not generalize a provider's enterprise/API commitment to consumer products or another account tier. Do not infer residency from company headquarters or endpoint hostname. Do not call data `zero retention`, `not trained on`, `Australian-hosted`, `EU-only` or similar without current applicable evidence.

## Revalidation
Re-check on provider/product/tier/region/contract/API changes or material policy updates.

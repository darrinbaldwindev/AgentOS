# SOP-RETIRE-001 — Worker and Project Retirement

Status: DRAFT / IMPLEMENTATION-DEPENDENT
Owner: SOP Overseer

## Purpose
Retire a worker, Overseer role, capability or project without erasing evidence, leaving live authority behind or silently redirecting canonical ownership.

## Pre-retirement
Inventory active missions/tasks, approvals, schedules, credentials/capabilities, locks/claims, data stores, repositories/branches, external integrations, budgets, unresolved incidents, Green/PRS evidence, retention/legal obligations and successor ownership.

## Procedure
1. Mark retirement intent and effective boundary.
2. Stop new assignment/admission through canonical routing.
3. Reconcile active work: complete safely, hand off explicitly or enter recovery/block state.
4. Revoke/expire role-specific approvals and future scheduling eligibility.
5. Revoke credentials/capabilities only through authorised secret/capability procedures.
6. Release or recover locks/claims with evidence; never delete uncertain ownership evidence to make retirement look clean.
7. Preserve durable receipts, audit records and required legal records.
8. Transfer canonical project ownership/state only through the existing control plane.
9. Update documentation/registry references to `RETIRED`, `SUPERSEDED` or `ARCHIVED` as appropriate.
10. Verify no new work can be admitted under the retired identity/scope.

## Identity rule
Stable historical identity remains attributable in old evidence. Retirement does not rewrite prior receipts to a successor persona/worker.

## Project archive/delete
Archive and delete are distinct. Repository deletion, production data deletion, domain/store shutdown, account cancellation and customer/regulator communication are protected actions requiring explicit authority.

## Completion packet
Record retired identity/project, effective time, final active-work disposition, revoked access, retained evidence/data, successor if any, unresolved blockers and independent verification where required.

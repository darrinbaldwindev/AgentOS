# SOP-COM-004 — Supplier Onboarding

Status: DRAFT / IMPLEMENTATION-DEPENDENT

## Rule
Supplier research identifies candidates. Onboarding establishes an approved, bounded commercial relationship only after evidence and authorised human actions. A catalogue/API/feed/integration is not supplier approval.

## Onboarding packet
Supplier legal identity, trading names, jurisdiction, contact provenance, account/contract status, products/categories, stock/origin, pricing/tax, freight/fulfilment, returns/warranty, service levels, dropship/marketplace permissions, integration scopes, data handling, payment terms, insurance/compliance evidence where applicable, product-safety responsibilities, recall/contact process, IP/brand permissions, evidence dates, owner, approval state.

## Procedure
1. Promote only evidence-qualified research candidates.
2. Verify identity and material commercial terms.
3. Resolve freight/landed-cost UNKNOWNs before margin/free-delivery approval.
4. Resolve product safety/importer/marketplace responsibilities.
5. Review contract/account terms and required legal/commercial approvals.
6. Connect integrations with least privilege under MCP/capability/credential controls.
7. Test data/feed quality without production publication.
8. Record approved scope and revalidation triggers.

Supplier contact, account creation, contractual acceptance, purchase, payment, credential changes and production listing activation remain protected actions unless specifically authorised.

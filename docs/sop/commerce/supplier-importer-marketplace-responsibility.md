# SOP-COM-002 — Supplier, Importer and Marketplace Responsibility Gate

Status: DRAFT / LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED / JURISDICTION-DEPENDENT
Owner: SOP Overseer

## Purpose
Prevent GlobalShopCo, Shopify/eBay/Amazon workstreams, MyPrimeDelivery and other commerce projects from treating supplier or marketplace availability as proof that the portfolio may lawfully/safely sell, import, advertise or fulfil a product.

## Core rule
Supplier listing, marketplace acceptance, AU-stock representation, dropship availability or an integration feed is evidence about a source/channel only. It does not transfer or eliminate the operator's legal, safety, consumer, tax, import, warranty, recall, IP or advertising responsibilities.

## Required responsibility packet
For each product/category before commercial activation record: operator/seller identity; jurisdiction/customer market; supplier legal identity; manufacturer/brand; importer/responsible-person role where applicable; product category; exact SKU/variant; origin/ship-from; safety standards/bans/mandatory standards applicability; recalls; labelling/warnings; electrical/battery/chemical/children/food/therapeutic or other regulated characteristics; IP/brand authorization; dropship/marketplace permission; stock/lead time; landed/freight basis; returns/warranty allocation; consumer-remedy owner; insurance requirement if any; evidence sources/dates; UNKNOWNs; approval state.

## Fail closed
Hold/reject activation when a material safety/ban/recall/compliance role is UNKNOWN, a supplier identity is unverifiable, marketplace permission is absent where required, product claims depend on unsupported certification, or fulfilment/returns responsibilities are contradictory.

## Marketplace boundary
Shopify remains canonical commerce authority where the project architecture says so. eBay/Amazon/WordPress/affiliate presentation cannot manufacture seller approval, safety compliance, stock, freight or margin truth.

## Automation
AI workers may research and assemble evidence but cannot contact suppliers, accept contracts, purchase, activate listings, make customs/regulatory declarations or publish compliance claims without explicit authority.

This SOP coordinates evidence and review; it is not legal/import/product-safety certification.

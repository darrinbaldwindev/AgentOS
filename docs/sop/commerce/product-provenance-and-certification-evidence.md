# Product Provenance and Certification Evidence Gate

**Document ID:** SOP-COM-008
**Status:** DRAFT / IMPLEMENTATION-DEPENDENT / LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

## Purpose
Keep supplier/product claims bound to exact evidence rather than marketplace reputation or copied listing text.

## Evidence packet
Bind exact SKU/variant/model, brand/manufacturer, supplier identity, source listing/document, country/market, origin/manufacture claims if made, batch/lot where relevant, certificate/test-report identifier, issuing body/lab, scope/standard, validity dates, warnings/instructions, chain-of-custody or supplier linkage and verification date.

## Rules
- A certificate for a related model does not prove the target SKU.
- Supplier assertion is evidence of the assertion, not independent certification.
- Logo/mark imagery is not proof of valid certification.
- Marketplace presence or bestseller status is not safety/compliance proof.
- Expired, unverifiable, mismatched or materially incomplete evidence => HOLD/UNKNOWN.

## Publication
Do not publish certification, origin, safety, sustainability or compliance claims beyond what the evidence supports. Production listing changes remain protected actions.

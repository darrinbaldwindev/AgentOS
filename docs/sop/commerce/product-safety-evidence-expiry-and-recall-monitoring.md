# Product-Safety Evidence Expiry and Recall Monitoring

**Document ID:** SOP-COM-007
**Status:** DRAFT / IMPLEMENTATION-DEPENDENT / LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

## Principle
A past safety check is not permanent proof that a SKU remains safe, lawful, unrecalled or correctly supplied.

## Evidence record
Bind exact SKU/variant, brand/model identifiers, supplier, market/jurisdiction, evidence source, checked date, applicable standards/bans/recalls/warnings, certificates/test reports where relevant, expiry/review date and reviewer.

## Revalidation triggers
Re-check when supplier or SKU changes; a certificate/report expires; regulator/recall information changes; product composition/packaging/warnings change; complaints or incidents indicate safety risk; marketplace status changes; or the review cadence expires.

## State
`CURRENT`, `REVALIDATION_DUE`, `SAFETY_HOLD`, `RECALLED/PROHIBITED`, `UNKNOWN`.

UNKNOWN, conflicting evidence, an applicable recall/prohibition or materially stale safety evidence blocks publication/restock/renewed promotion until resolved.

## Authority
Research workers may detect and record signals. They may not autonomously issue a legal recall, contact regulators/suppliers/customers, destroy stock, admit liability or alter a production listing without authority.

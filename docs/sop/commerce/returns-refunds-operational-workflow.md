# Returns and Refunds Operational Workflow

**Document ID:** SOP-COM-006
**Status:** DRAFT / IMPLEMENTATION-DEPENDENT / LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

## Purpose
Provide an operational evidence flow for commerce returns/refunds while preserving statutory-rights and jurisdiction gates.

## Intake record
Capture order/transaction identity, operator/store, jurisdiction, item/service, customer request, dates, stated reason, delivery evidence, product condition evidence where relevant, supplier/marketplace involvement, payment path and applicable published policy version.

## Decision boundary
Published store policy does not override non-excludable statutory rights. Classify whether the request may involve defect, safety, misdescription, non-delivery, change-of-mind or another basis; if legal applicability is uncertain, route to review rather than denying rights.

## Execution
A worker may prepare evidence and a proposed remedy only within authority. Refund issuance, payment reversal, supplier/customer contact, admission, settlement or policy exception are external/financial actions requiring the applicable authority.

## Evidence
Record decision basis, approver where required, exact amount/method, external transaction identifier, communication evidence and final state. A payment API response must be scoped to what it actually proves.

## Safety
Potential product-safety matters also enter the safety/recall workflow; a refund alone does not close a safety obligation.

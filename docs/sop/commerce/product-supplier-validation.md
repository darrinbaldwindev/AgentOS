# SOP-COM-001 — Product and Supplier Validation

**Status:** DRAFT / PORTFOLIO-DERIVED  
**Last verified:** 2026-09-15

## Purpose
Standardise evidence-gated product research for commerce projects without converting candidates into approved suppliers/SKUs prematurely.

## Evidence classes
Record each material field as VERIFIED FACT, REASONABLE INFERENCE or UNKNOWN. A supplier candidate is not an approved supplier; public catalogue availability is not account-level trade availability; demand evidence is not margin evidence.

## Minimum SKU evidence
Capture product identity/SKU, supplier identity, source URL/date, item/trade cost status, GST/tax treatment where relevant, stock/availability, fulfilment model, dispatch origin, freight/delivery cost by relevant destinations, marketplace/platform fees, payment/integration costs, target sell-price evidence, delivered gross margin, free-delivery viability, returns/warranty, compliance/safety constraints and integration path.

## Gate
Reject or hold any candidate whose viability depends on an UNKNOWN material cost—especially freight—or unsupported account/trade pricing. Do not claim positive margin by substituting optimistic estimates for missing supplier evidence.

## Production boundary
Research/validation does not authorise supplier contact, account acceptance, purchase, app installation, Shopify/eBay production writes, listing publication or spend.

## Canonical commerce authority
Where Shopify is designated product/variant/pricing/inventory/cart/checkout/order authority, downstream headless/marketplace layers must consume that truth rather than becoming a second commerce database.

# SOP-COM-005 — Subscription, Billing and Cancellation Evidence Gate

Status: DRAFT / IMPLEMENTATION-DEPENDENT / LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED
Owner: SOP Overseer

## Core rule
Pricing direction is not billing implementation. Do not publish purchase, renewal, cancellation, refund, tax or entitlement behavior until the exact commerce stack and jurisdiction-specific terms are evidenced and reviewed.

## Current product-direction baseline
AgentOS Free — $0/year; AgentOS Standard — $49/year; AgentOS Advanced / Pro — $99/year; AI Plus — $22/month as a separate optional intelligence subscription; Commercial / Business — TBD. These are PRODUCT DIRECTION unless current implementation evidence establishes sellable plans/entitlements.

## Evidence packet before activation
Record operator/seller, jurisdiction/currency/tax treatment, plan/SKU, price, billing interval, trial, renewal behavior, payment processor, checkout surface, entitlement mapping, upgrade/downgrade proration, cancellation effective time, access after cancellation, refund/statutory-rights handling, failed payment/dunning, receipts/invoices, data handling, support route, terms/privacy versions, exact implementation/release and approvals.

## Fail closed
Do not infer live billing from a pricing page, payment-provider account, API key, mock checkout, plan constant or product copy. Do not advertise `cancel anytime`, instant refunds, annual savings, tax-inclusive pricing, renewal timing or entitlement behavior unless current implementation and applicable terms support it.

## Separation
Commercial entitlement does not increase governance authority. AI Plus changes available intelligence/capability only as implemented; it cannot bypass permissions, budgets, approvals, Green or PRS.

## Cancellation
Cancellation UX must distinguish stopping future renewal, entitlement end, refunds, data deletion and connector/provider subscription cancellation. These are separate actions/evidence.

No live billing or subscription activation is authorised by this SOP.

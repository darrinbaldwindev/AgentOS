# AgentOS Product Data Learning & Licensing Model

## Product-data learning

AgentOS should support a privacy-first Product Learning and Discovery capability that can use appropriately consented, minimised and anonymised operational data to improve AgentOS and identify opportunities for new software.

The system should distinguish safe aggregate operational telemetry from user content. Sensitive content such as conversations, private repositories, customer information, source documents and credentials must not automatically enter product-learning datasets.

Potential aggregate signals include task type, success/failure, duration, worker/provider selected, capability usage, cost/credit efficiency, error categories, workflow patterns and feature usage.

The learning/discovery pipeline should support three outcomes:

1. **AgentOS Learning Engine** — improve routing, workflows, prompts, reliability and capability selection.
2. **Capability Discovery Engine** — identify recurring unmet needs and propose new capabilities/Skill Agents.
3. **Product Discovery Engine** — identify sufficiently recurring unmet needs that may justify new standalone software.

Commercial relationships must not determine product-learning conclusions or provider selection.

## Privacy and consent

Product-learning collection must be explicit, transparent, minimised and governed by consent and applicable privacy requirements. Users should be able to control participation where applicable. AgentOS must avoid collecting secrets or unnecessary private content for product development.

## Software licensing / update model

Proposed consumer product model:

- **AgentOS Free** — separate cut-down installation.
- **AgentOS 2.1** — $29 one-time purchase.
- **AgentOS 2.3** — $99 one-time purchase and the full AgentOS product.

The paid software purchase is not a monthly software subscription.

### Proposed 12-month update entitlement

A paid AgentOS purchase includes **12 months of software updates** from purchase/activation.

After the included 12-month update period, the installed program should continue to operate. The customer does not lose the software they purchased merely because they do not renew updates.

An optional **$99 annual update renewal** may provide another 12 months of updates and improvements. Renewal should be voluntary and should not disable the previously purchased software.

The renewal should be treated as an update/maintenance entitlement, not as a requirement to keep the core program functional.

Exact legal/licensing wording, renewal pricing, grace periods and whether security fixes are supplied outside an active update entitlement remain product decisions to be validated before launch.

## AI/service subscriptions remain separate

External AI/model/agent subscriptions are separate from the AgentOS software purchase. They may be monthly subscriptions and may be recommended temporarily when a project materially benefits from a specialist capability.

AgentOS may recommend a one-month external subscription, prepare provider-specific work in advance, measure utility and recommend cancellation when the capability is no longer justified.

Affiliate/referral revenue must never determine the recommendation.

## Strategic principle

**Buy AgentOS once. Choose ongoing AI services as needed. Keep AgentOS useful even when external subscriptions change.**

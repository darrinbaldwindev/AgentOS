## Hero

### Share AgentOS. Earn when your audience becomes a customer.

If you create useful content about AI, productivity, technology, small business or everyday computing, the AgentOS Affiliate Program gives you a structured way to recommend AgentOS and earn commission on qualifying purchases.

AgentOS is built around a simple idea: **Put AI to work.**

Your job as an affiliate is not to oversell that promise. It is to explain where AgentOS fits, show real use cases, disclose your commercial relationship clearly, and let your audience decide.

## Why promote AgentOS

- Clear product story: help people get more from the AI they already use.
- Broad audience: everyday users, creators, professionals, small businesses and technical users.
- Multiple tiers: Free, Co-worker and Operator.
- Low annual entry points compared with many recurring AI subscriptions.
- Content-friendly use cases: real jobs, demos, comparisons, workflow improvement, free/local/paid AI coordination.
- Creator-friendly positioning: useful education first, hard sell second.

## How it works

1. Apply and tell us about your audience and channels.
2. If approved, receive your affiliate link, approved messaging and disclosure guide.
3. Create honest content in your own voice.
4. Place a clear affiliate disclosure close to the recommendation or link.
5. Earn commission on qualifying purchases attributed to your link under the current program rules.

## What we want from affiliates

We want affiliates who:
- understand their audience;
- explain products honestly;
- demonstrate real use where possible;
- disclose commercial relationships clearly;
- avoid misleading savings, performance or feature claims;
- respect email, privacy, advertising and platform rules;
- do not pressure or mislead people into buying.

## What is not allowed

The program does not permit:
- spam or unsolicited bulk messaging;
- scraped or purchased contact lists;
- cookie stuffing, forced clicks or attribution hijacking;
- fake reviews, fake testimonials or fabricated scarcity;
- pretending to be AgentOS or an official AgentOS account;
- self-referral schemes or manufactured transactions;
- misleading product claims;
- bidding on restricted brand/trademark keywords where prohibited by the program rules;
- unapproved coupon, cashback, browser-extension or sub-affiliate traffic;
- content that hides or obscures the commercial relationship.

## Required disclosure

A simple baseline disclosure is:

> **Ad / Affiliate:** I may earn a commission if you purchase AgentOS through this link, at no extra cost to you.

The disclosure must be clear, prominent and appropriate to the channel. Do not rely on a buried terms page or a profile bio alone.

## FAQ teaser

### Do I need a huge audience?
No. Relevance, trust and content quality matter more than raw follower count.

### Can I use my own words?
Yes. You should. But factual claims still need to match current approved AgentOS evidence and program guidance.

### Can I compare AgentOS with other AI products?
Yes, if the comparison is accurate, current and fair. Do not invent weaknesses, prices or capabilities of competitors.

### Can I run paid ads?
Only if the final program rules permit the method and any brand/trademark restrictions are followed.

### Can I email my audience?
Only where you have the required consent and comply with applicable marketing laws and platform/provider rules.

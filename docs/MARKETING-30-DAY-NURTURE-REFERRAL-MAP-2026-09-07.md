# AgentOS 30-Day Nurture & Referral Message Map

**Owner:** Marketing Overseer  
**Date:** 2026-09-07  
**Status:** RECOMMENDATION / VALIDATION REQUIRED

## Purpose

Convert the current commercial direction into a practical first-30-day nurture sequence that introduces Willow, Isla, Jack and Henry quickly, demonstrates value through real tasks, supports Free → $39/year → $99/year progression, and creates a non-intrusive member referral loop.

This is a marketing/product hypothesis. It does not activate pricing, subscriptions, notifications, referral rewards, or runtime autonomy.

## Commercial working model

- **Free:** limited but genuinely useful taste.
- **$39/year:** preferred challenger to $29/year; positioned as the low-friction Co-worker tier at roughly $0.75/week.
- **$99/year:** Operator destination at roughly $1.90/week; broader autonomy, capacity, integrations, recurring work and Night Shift scope.
- **Additional subscriptions / BYO AI:** for users needing extra models, specialist capability, higher usage, business functionality or external providers.

$39 remains a **test candidate**, not approved final pricing. $29 remains the control in validation.

## Governing onboarding rule

The first 30 days are a **nurture window**, not a calendar-gated feature unlock.

Character introductions should be primarily **task-triggered** and should all occur within the first week for an active user.

If a user naturally reaches a relevant moment earlier, introduce the relevant AgentOS role immediately.

Do not force users to wait for a scheduled day.

## Character role integrity

Character roles must remain stable:

- **Willow — Understands and plans.**
- **Isla — Executes.**
- **Jack — Controls authority, permissions and risk.**
- **Henry — Checks, verifies and assures.**

Do not turn the characters into feature categories such as “Willow = research” or “Henry = Night Shift.” All four may participate in the same workflow.

## Week 1 — Introduce the whole team

### First meaningful job — Willow

Trigger: user submits a task requiring more than a trivial answer.

Suggested message:

> **Willow:** I help AgentOS understand what you want and work out the plan.

Show the plan briefly, then continue. Do not interrupt task completion with a long introduction.

Marketing objective: establish that AgentOS plans work rather than merely replying.

Upgrade education only when relevant:

> With Co-worker, AgentOS can handle more complex and repeatable jobs like this.

### First real execution — Isla

Trigger: AgentOS actually performs a multi-step action, research process, file operation, browser task, or connected workflow.

Suggested message:

> **Isla:** I’m the part of AgentOS that gets the work done.

Marketing objective: demonstrate execution, not another chatbot persona.

Relevant upgrade education:

> Co-worker gives AgentOS more capacity and more ways to complete jobs for you.

### First authority/permission boundary — Jack

Trigger: a workflow requires approval, access, spend, an external write, or another governed action.

Suggested message:

> **Jack:** I make sure AgentOS stays within the limits you set.

Marketing objective: turn a permission interruption into a trust-building moment.

Relevant upgrade education:

> Paid plans can connect more of your tools while keeping the same approval and safety controls.

### First assured result — Henry

Trigger: AgentOS checks evidence, sources, outputs, completion state or task criteria.

Suggested message:

> **Henry:** I check the work before AgentOS calls it finished.

Marketing objective: show evidence/assurance as a differentiator.

Relevant upgrade education:

> Operator can apply this checking to larger, recurring and autonomous jobs.

## Days 8–30 — Reinforcement, not repeated introductions

After all four roles have been introduced, stop re-explaining who they are.

Instead, use them contextually:

- Willow highlights repeatable patterns or better delegation opportunities.
- Isla shows when more execution capacity would help.
- Jack explains new approval or autonomy controls when encountered.
- Henry surfaces proof, evidence and readiness for more complex work.

## Upgrade message rules

Upgrade prompts should be **value-triggered**, not fixed-frequency sales popups during the first 30 days.

High-value trigger examples:

1. User repeats the same task multiple times.
2. User attempts a multi-step workflow beyond Free limits.
3. User reaches a capacity/usage boundary.
4. User tries to connect another service.
5. User asks for memory/persistence that exceeds Free.
6. User attempts recurring or scheduled work.
7. User expresses interest in Night Shift / Autonomy Hours.
8. User requests broader AI/model capability.
9. User completes several successful tasks and demonstrates recurring value.

## $39/year working message

Preferred value framing for test:

> **Get much more from AgentOS for less than $1/week.**

Support with concrete value:

- more jobs
- more AI capability
- broader free-AI capability pool
- more complex work
- more memory/context
- more integrations
- limited scheduled delegation / Night Shift
- higher capacity

Do not make $39 simply “$99 but with fewer clicks.”

## $99/year working message

Preferred value framing:

> **Let AgentOS take more work off your hands.**

Support with:

- substantially broader Autonomy Hours
- multiple Night Shift routines
- recurring jobs
- larger queues
- more Context Space autonomy
- broader integrations
- higher AI/resource allowance
- more parallel and complex work
- stronger checkpoint/recovery capability

## Night Shift nurture

Night Shift should be introduced only after AgentOS has already demonstrated useful interactive work and basic trust.

Suggested pre-activation concept:

> **Tonight’s Shift**
> Willow shows the plan.
> Jack shows what is allowed and what still needs approval.
> Isla carries out authorised work.
> Henry checks the results.
> The user receives a Morning Brief.

$39 should be allowed to demonstrate a limited Night Shift experience so it can create desire for $99 rather than hiding the core autonomy concept completely.

## Day 30 checkpoint

At approximately Day 30, present a concise personal value summary rather than a generic paywall.

Potential facts, only when genuinely measured:

- jobs completed
- repeat jobs detected
- files/documents processed
- connected services used
- scheduled work attempted
- approvals handled
- results assured
- Free/premium AI usage where accurately available

Then offer the most relevant next tier.

## Post-Day-30 weekly upgrade cadence

After Day 30, a reminder may occur approximately every seven days **only when there is a fresh user-specific value reason**.

Examples:

- “You manually ran this task three times. Operator could run it on your schedule.”
- “You’ve hit your Free job allowance twice this month. Co-worker gives you more capacity.”
- “You’ve used AgentOS for research six times. Co-worker can save this as a repeatable workflow.”

### Suppression rules

Do not show a reminder when:

- no new value signal exists
- user dismissed the same message recently
- user is in the middle of a critical task
- user has explicitly indicated they do not want upgrade prompts
- a payment/upgrade attempt recently failed or is already in progress
- the message would simply repeat the previous week’s wording

The intent is **helpful progression, not nagging**.

## Member referral programme

Member referrals are separate from the creator/affiliate programme.

### Referral goal

Create natural word-of-mouth after proven usefulness.

Desired user sentiment:

> “Instead of figuring out which AI to use, I just give AgentOS the job.”

Official marketing should not claim “the best way to access AI models” without evidence. Safer working language:

> **A simpler way to access and use AI.**

or

> **AgentOS brings your AI together and helps you make the most of it.**

### Referral trigger

Do not ask immediately after signup.

Prompt only after demonstrated value, such as:

- multiple completed jobs
- a successful Night Shift
- a meaningful time-saving workflow
- repeated weekly use
- positive feedback
- an explicit share/recommend intent signal

Suggested message:

> **Finding AgentOS useful? Know someone who would too?**

### Referral reward candidates to test

Non-cash rewards may provide high perceived value at lower economic cost:

- additional AI allowance
- extra Night Shift runs
- temporary Operator access
- account credit
- additional specialist capability
- temporary higher limits

All reward structures require abuse prevention and unit-economics validation before activation.

## Creator vs member referral separation

### Creator / Affiliate programme

For publishers, influencers, reviewers and commercial partners.

Working commercial hypothesis remains separate and may include tracked attribution and commission.

### Member referral programme

For ordinary users recommending AgentOS to friends, family or colleagues.

Use simple reciprocal value rather than complex affiliate economics unless a later test proves cash rewards are preferable.

## Primary messaging hierarchy

### Top of funnel

> **Put AI to work.**

### Consideration

> **You already have AI. AgentOS helps you make the most of it.**

### Product command box

> **Tell AgentOS what you want done…**

These messages serve different funnel jobs and should not be forced into a single winner prematurely.

## Validation requirements

Before locking this sequence, test:

1. $29 vs $39 first-paid conversion.
2. $39 effect on eventual $99 progression.
3. Whether all four character introductions in Week 1 improve or reduce comprehension.
4. Whether task-triggered onboarding outperforms calendar-gated onboarding.
5. Whether limited $39 Night Shift increases $99 desire or cannibalises it.
6. Weekly reminder tolerance and suppression effectiveness.
7. Referral prompt timing.
8. Reward preference and abuse risk.
9. Whether users naturally describe AgentOS as an easier way to use multiple AI capabilities.
10. Whether “Put AI to work” improves acquisition without creating unsupported autonomy expectations.

# AgentOS Affiliate Promotion Wording & Disclosure Guide

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Status:** DRAFT FOR OWNER + LEGAL REVIEW

## Purpose

Give affiliates usable wording that is persuasive, clear and legally safer without forcing them to invent claims or disclosures.

## 1. Required relationship disclosure

Use plain language that explains the commercial relationship.

### Preferred universal form

> **Ad / Affiliate:** I may earn a commission if you purchase AgentOS through this link, at no extra cost to you.

### Short social form

> **Ad — affiliate link. I may earn a commission.**

### Review/blog form

> This post contains affiliate links. If you purchase AgentOS through one of these links, I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. My views are my own.

### Video form

Spoken near the start:

> This video includes an affiliate link for AgentOS, so I may earn a commission if you buy through it.

Also place a visible disclosure on screen and in the description where practical.

## 2. Wording hierarchy

### Primary approved-style promise

> **Put AI to work.**

### Supporting approved-style lines

- You already have AI. AgentOS helps you make the most of it.
- Use free AI. Use local AI. Use the AI you already pay for. AgentOS helps put it to work together.
- Instead of figuring out which AI to use, give AgentOS the job.
- One platform. Every AI.
- AgentOS is designed to coordinate the AI available to you based on the job, your preferences and your limits.

## 3. Safer affiliate review wording

### Good

> AgentOS is designed to coordinate multiple AI providers and tools so you can focus on the job instead of manually choosing and managing every AI step.

### Good

> One of the ideas I like is the free-first approach: AgentOS is designed to use free, local or existing AI where suitable and escalate only within your rules and limits.

### Good

> The $99/year Operator tier is positioned as the plan for people who want more scheduled and autonomous work, while keeping the same governance and safety boundaries.

### Avoid until evidence is promoted

- “AgentOS always picks the best AI.”
- “AgentOS is always cheaper.”
- “AgentOS will save you more than $99 a year.”
- “AgentOS replaces all your AI subscriptions.”
- “AgentOS works with every AI.”
- “AgentOS can do anything on your computer.”
- “AgentOS works overnight even when your computer is off.”
- “Guaranteed savings.”
- “Guaranteed productivity gains.”

## 4. Pricing wording

### Safe baseline

> AgentOS Operator is $99/year — less than $2 a week.

### Safe value framing

> The idea behind Operator is simple: if it saves you even a small amount of time or unnecessary AI spend each week, the annual price can be easy to justify.

### Do not say

> It will definitely pay for itself.

until measured evidence supports that claim and the Marketing Claims Register is updated.

## 5. Free-AI wording

### Safe now

> AgentOS is designed to make better use of free, local and paid AI rather than assuming every job needs another paid subscription.

### Benchmark-gated stronger wording

Only after the Free-AI Effectiveness Benchmark clears the stated gate:

> In our controlled benchmark, AgentOS completed [X] of [Y] representative jobs using only eligible free/local AI.

Do not invent the numbers.

## 6. Comparison wording

Affiliates may compare AgentOS with other products if the comparison is factual, current, fair and based on comparable features.

Preferred style:

> ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini are powerful AI products. AgentOS is positioned differently: it is designed to coordinate the AI and tools available to you around a job.

Avoid:
- “better than ChatGPT” without a defined test;
- “cheaper than every competitor”;
- misleading feature checklists;
- outdated competitor pricing;
- implying official partnerships unless they actually exist.

## 8. Mandatory behaviour for affiliates

Affiliates must:
- keep disclosures close to endorsements and links;
- identify ads/affiliate relationships before consumers act;
- update stale pricing/features;
- distinguish personal opinion from factual product claims;
- use current approved claims guidance;
- correct inaccurate content promptly;
- disclose gifted/free access where that relationship could affect how the audience interprets the endorsement.

## 9. Channel-specific notes

### Australia
Use clear commercial disclosure and ensure factual claims are truthful and supportable. Do not rely on vague labels that could hide the commercial relationship.

### United States
Clear and conspicuous disclosure should be placed with the endorsement. “Affiliate link” alone may not always explain to consumers that the promoter earns money; plain-language commission wording is preferred.

### United Kingdom
Affiliate marketing is advertising. Use an obvious identifier such as **Ad** early enough that the commercial nature is clear before or at engagement.

## 10. Creator freedom

Affiliates should not be forced to give positive reviews. They may express genuine criticism or mixed views. The program should require truthfulness and disclosure, not scripted praise.

This protects credibility and reduces risk from endorsements that do not reflect the creator's genuine experience.

# AgentOS — Marketing Proof & Measurement Plan — 2026-09-03

**Owner:** Marketing Overseer  
**Status:** READY FOR IMPLEMENTATION-EVIDENCE CHECK  
**Scope:** Marketing validation only.

## Objective

When the first currently implemented governed workflow is identified, capture enough evidence to determine whether it supports a credible commercial story.

## Proof sequence

1. Starting condition and business problem.
2. Human/system trigger.
3. Workers, models or systems actually used.
4. Policy, permission and approval boundaries actually enforced.
5. Work performed and observable outputs.
6. Challenge/review evidence.
7. Assurance/evidence record.
8. Human acceptance or governed completion.
9. Failure/exception path.
10. Measurable baseline/result where available.

## Marketing measurements

- Time from trigger to acceptance.
- Human interventions and approval points.
- Exceptions requiring escalation.
- Rework or rejected outputs.
- Evidence completeness.
- Workflow completion rate.
- User/operator satisfaction.
- Trial/adoption intent.
- Commercial willingness-to-pay signal.

These measurements are proposed measurement fields, not claims that AgentOS currently records them.

## Claim gate

A buyer-facing claim requires both:

- implementation evidence showing the claimed workflow/capability exists; and
- customer/problem evidence showing the capability matters.

Market research alone cannot satisfy either gate.

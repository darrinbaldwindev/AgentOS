# AgentOS — Marketing Wedge Test Plan — 2026-09-03

**Owner:** Marketing Overseer  
**Status:** HYPOTHESIS / READY FOR IMPLEMENTATION-EVIDENCE CHECK  
**Scope:** Marketing validation only. This document does not authorise runtime, integration, credential or production changes.

## Purpose

Turn the current AgentOS commercial wedge hypothesis into one falsifiable demonstration test.

## Wedge under test

**Governed cross-system exception resolution.**

The test should demonstrate, using only capabilities actually implemented and evidenced in AgentOS:

1. identify an exception/task;
2. analyse it with an AI worker;
3. coordinate or plan the required work;
4. execute only permitted actions;
5. challenge/review the result;
6. capture assurance/evidence;
7. deliver an acceptance-ready result to a human or governed downstream process.

## Candidate buyer

Primary hypothesis: AI/platform/operations leaders coordinating multiple AI workers/providers across business workflows.

Secondary hypothesis: engineering/project leaders responsible for accepting AI-assisted or autonomous work and needing evidence of what ran and what was checked.

## Test requirements

Before public positioning, implementation owners must identify one existing workflow/integration that can actually be demonstrated. Marketing must not invent the integration, action permissions, autonomy level or outcome.

The demonstration should capture:

- starting condition;
- systems/workers involved;
- permitted actions;
- approval gates;
- challenge/review evidence;
- assurance evidence;
- final output;
- failure/exception handling;
- measurable baseline and result where feasible.

## Market context

Current 2026 research reinforces the relevance of orchestration, governance, control-plane authority and oversight for multi-agent environments. This is category evidence, not AgentOS capability evidence.

## Decision rule

Do not convert the wedge into a public product claim until the implementation evidence and customer/problem evidence both pass. If the workflow cannot be demonstrated with current AgentOS capability, replace the wedge rather than stretching the claim.

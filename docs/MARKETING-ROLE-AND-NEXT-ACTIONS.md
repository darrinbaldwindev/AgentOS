# Marketing Role & Next Actions

**Owner:** Marketing Overseer
**Status:** Active portfolio marketing workstream
**Date:** 2026-09-03

## Role
Marketing owns product positioning, ICP/customer research, messaging, GTM, acquisition, activation, retention, conversion, SEO/AEO, content, competitive intelligence, commercial experiments, and marketing verification. It does not alter AgentOS runtime, permissions, scheduling, governance, credentials or production infrastructure.

## Current marketing requirements

1. Establish evidence-backed AgentOS positioning and differentiation.
2. Define ICPs, priority use cases and customer journeys.
3. Turn the AI Team and governed workflow into demonstrable product marketing assets without treating mockups as implementation evidence.
4. Build SEO/AEO information architecture and authoritative public-facing content.
5. Define acquisition → activation → retained usage → paid conversion loops.
6. Validate commercial claims, pricing/value hypotheses and competitor comparisons.
7. Maintain a proof library mapping every material claim to evidence.

## Immediate actions

- Prioritise governed multi-model/multi-agent execution as the core differentiation hypothesis.
- Build proof-led demos around Understand → Plan → Execute → Challenge → Assure → Deliver.
- Treat Simple / Essentials / Tech Head as packaging/experience communication, not separate capability claims.
- Keep third-party AI capabilities explicitly evidence-classified.
- Identify the first customer segment where cross-system governed execution produces measurable value.

## Gate
No production marketing claim should be published as fact until implementation and evidence exist.

# AgentOS — ICP & Commercial Wedge Validation — 2026-09-03

**Owner:** Marketing Overseer  
**Status:** HYPOTHESIS / VALIDATION REQUIRED  
**Scope:** Commercial positioning only; no runtime or architecture authority.

## Objective

Identify one narrow customer/problem wedge that can be tested with a demonstrable governed workflow before broad AgentOS positioning is promoted.

## Candidate ICP

**Primary hypothesis:** AI/platform/operations leaders responsible for deploying or coordinating multiple AI agents, models or AI-enabled workflows across business systems.

**Secondary hypothesis:** engineering/project leaders who must accept autonomous or AI-assisted work but need stronger evidence of what ran, what was checked, and whether the result is ready to accept.

These are hypotheses, not validated customer segments.

## Candidate problem

A business may have multiple AI workers, providers and business-system workflows but lack a single governed operating layer for coordinating work, applying appropriate controls, challenging results and producing evidence for acceptance.

This is supported as a market problem signal, not as proof of AgentOS product-market fit.

## Candidate wedge

### Governed cross-system exception resolution

Test a workflow in which:

1. A business-system exception or task is identified.
2. An AI worker analyses the issue.
3. A planner assigns/coordinates work.
4. An executor performs an allowed action.
5. A challenge/review step tests the result.
6. Assurance records evidence.
7. A human or governed process accepts the outcome.

The exact integration and action set must be selected from currently implemented AgentOS capabilities. No integration is assumed by this document.

## Why this wedge

It tests the proposed differentiation directly: coordination + governance + challenge + assurance across heterogeneous workers/systems, rather than another generic chat or agent builder.

## Competitive baseline

Current 2026 market signals show increasing attention to AI governance, agent management, orchestration, agent inventory/sprawl, cost controls and cross-platform operation. This validates the category direction but does not establish AgentOS superiority.

## Validation questions

- Who currently owns this problem?
- How often does the workflow occur?
- What happens when the workflow is wrong or incomplete?
- What evidence is required before acceptance?
- Which systems must participate?
- Which actions may be autonomous versus approval-gated?
- What is the current cost in time, rework or risk?
- What existing products/workflows are used?
- Would a provider-independent control layer be materially useful?
- What would justify paid adoption?

## Pass criteria

Do not advance the wedge to validated positioning unless evidence demonstrates:

- a clearly identifiable buyer/user;
- a recurring, consequential workflow;
- a current pain or material operational gap;
- a workflow AgentOS can actually demonstrate;
- a measurable improvement or control benefit;
- credible willingness to trial/pay or equivalent strong adoption signal.

**UNKNOWN:** ICP strength, workflow frequency, willingness to pay, measurable customer outcome, competitive win rate and implementation completeness.

**NEXT:** validate one concrete workflow against real user/problem evidence, then reconcile the result with actual AgentOS implementation evidence before creating public claims.

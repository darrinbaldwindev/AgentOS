## Purpose

Turn the current AgentOS commercial positioning into a repeatable 30-day social publishing programme suitable for Content360 repurposing and scheduling.

Core commercial messages:

- AgentOS helps users make the most of free, local and paid AI.
- AgentOS is designed to reduce unnecessary AI spend and save time.
- Free is useful; $39 Co-worker is the low-friction paid step; $99 Operator is the recommended tier.
- $99/year is less than $2 per week.
- Willow plans, Isla executes, Jack governs, Henry assures.
- AgentOS is one governed system with different entitlement envelopes, not separate products.

## Channel roles

- **Facebook:** mainstream education, everyday use cases, family/home, longer explanations.
- **Instagram:** strong visuals, characters, short benefits, Reels.
- **TikTok:** hook-first demonstrations, challenges, before/after.
- **YouTube Shorts:** concise product demonstrations and real-job series.
- **LinkedIn:** professional, small-business, ROI, governance, workflow positioning.
- **X:** concise ideas, product principles, threads, benchmark progress.

Do not mechanically post identical copy to all channels. Content360 should repurpose each core idea to the native format.

## Weekly content mix

Target distribution:

- 30% education / explanation
- 20% real jobs / demonstrations
- 15% free-AI and cost-value content
- 15% character / trust content
- 10% $39 / $99 conversion content
- 10% product vision / Night Shift / hybrid capability

Avoid more than two consecutive direct sales posts.

## Repurposing rule

Each core post should produce up to five variants:

1. one short-form video concept;
2. one square or portrait static visual;
3. one short caption;
4. one longer Facebook/LinkedIn version;
5. one X post/thread angle.

Do not republish the same wording unchanged across networks.

## 30-day primary measurement

Track per post where available:

- impressions/reach;
- 3-second and completed video views;
- saves/shares;
- comments/questions;
- click-through;
- plan-page visits;
- Free-AI message engagement;
- Operator message engagement;
- audience/use-case resonance.

Do not infer conversion causality from engagement alone.

## Winning-message test

The first 30 days should determine which of these messages deserves hero-level prominence:

A. **Put AI to work.**
B. **Make the most of free AI.**
C. **Your AI team. Working for you.**
D. **Use the AI you already have.**
E. **$99/year — less than $2 a week.**

## Content360 operating boundary

Content360 may schedule, repurpose and distribute approved marketing content. It must not become the canonical AgentOS campaign strategy, mission scheduler, authority layer, content source of truth, or claims authority.

High-risk auto-comment or DM behaviour requires separate governance rules and should not be enabled merely because Content360 supports it.

## Claim gates

Before publishing, check the claims register.

Do not publish unqualified claims that AgentOS:

- always saves money;
- always chooses the best or cheapest model;
- replaces all AI subscriptions;
- already performs general Night Shift or remote unattended work;
- works with every AI provider;
- guarantees more than $99/year in savings.

Use evidence-safe wording such as:

- designed to make effective use of free AI;
- designed to reduce unnecessary AI spend;
- use free/local/paid AI where supported;
- $99/year is less than $2 per week;
- built to save time;
- AgentOS helps you make the most of the AI you already have.

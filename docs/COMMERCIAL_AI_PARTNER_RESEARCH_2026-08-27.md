# AgentOS Commercial AI Partner Research — 2026-08-27

## Purpose

Research the commercial opportunities for AgentOS to earn revenue from supported AI models/agents through legitimate affiliate, referral, partner, reseller, marketplace, and API-channel programs.

## Locked AgentOS commercial principle

AgentOS tiers do **not** downgrade model quality. Higher tiers provide access to more models/agents and more AgentOS capabilities. Model/provider access remains governed by the provider's own terms, the user's entitlement/API key, availability, quota and cost policy.

AgentOS revenue opportunities are intended to be:

1. AgentOS program sales.
2. AgentOS subscription tiers.
3. Affiliate/referral revenue from model and agent subscriptions where the provider permits it.
4. Eligible API referral/partner/reseller revenue where the provider permits it.
5. Marketplace/channel revenue where a formal partner/reseller structure exists.

## Key findings

### OpenAI

OpenAI launched the OpenAI Partner Network in June 2026. It supports partners that build, sell and deliver AI solutions with OpenAI, with co-selling and deployment motions and three partner tiers: Select, Advanced and Elite. OpenAI has not publicly published a simple self-serve affiliate commission rate in the material reviewed. Treat OpenAI as a **high-value strategic partner target**, not as a currently confirmed public affiliate commission source.

Sources: https://openai.com/business/partners/ ; https://openai.com/index/introducing-openai-partner-network/

### Anthropic / Claude

Anthropic launched the Claude Partner Network in March 2026 with an announced $100M 2026 investment. The June 2026 Services Track states that bringing new business is rewarded separately through referral credit and deal protection. The public material reviewed does not disclose a universal percentage commission. Treat Anthropic as a **strategic partner/referral/deal-registration target**, not as a confirmed public affiliate percentage.

Important: third-party affiliate directories conflict on whether Anthropic has a public affiliate program and on commission figures. Do not use those figures in financial forecasts until Anthropic confirms terms directly.

Sources: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-partner-network ; https://www.anthropic.com/news/services-track-partner-hub

### Google Cloud / Gemini

Google Cloud has a public affiliate program through CJ Affiliate. It pays a cash reward for each eligible new Google Cloud user, has no annual earnings cap, and offers performance-based/tiered payouts. However, the current official FAQ states the affiliate program is currently available to affiliates operating in the **US and Canada**, so it should not be assumed to be directly available to an Australian AgentOS entity. Google Cloud also has a broader Partner Network with AI/Gemini competencies and marketplace/channel opportunities.

Sources: https://cloud.google.com/affiliate-program ; https://cloud.google.com/partners

### Perplexity

Perplexity has documented referral and channel-partner activity. A current consumer referral program reviewed by Perplexity offered $15 cash to both referrer and friend for qualifying Comet referrals, but the terms stated that the program was US-only and could be changed or terminated. Perplexity's corporate filings also disclose affiliate relationships, reseller partnerships and referral agreements. Treat Perplexity as a **potential partner/referral target**, but do not assume the $15 consumer referral is an ongoing AgentOS affiliate program.

Sources: https://www.perplexity.ai/it/hub/legal/refer-a-friend-program ; Perplexity 2026 filing material.

### Groq

Third-party affiliate tracking currently reports a Groq affiliate program with a $15 one-time commission and 30-day cookie, but the tracked source also reports that the public program page returned 404. Another affiliate directory records the program as manual-approval and commission terms not publicly published. Therefore the $15 figure is **not sufficiently reliable for a forecast**. Groq remains a strong provider/partner target because of its cloud inference offering and AgentOS use case.

### OpenRouter

Third-party sources conflict. One current affiliate-tracking source says OpenRouter has no public affiliate/referral program; another community-maintained affiliate directory lists a program with usage-based referral credits but no published rate. Therefore AgentOS should currently model OpenRouter as **no confirmed affiliate revenue** until OpenRouter provides direct confirmation.

### AWS / Bedrock

AWS provides a substantially stronger formal channel opportunity. Amazon Bedrock became available for resale by AWS Channel Partners in October 2025 and includes models from multiple major providers, including Anthropic, Mistral and OpenAI, subject to provider limitations. AWS Marketplace also supports AI agents/tools and channel-partner private offers. Channel partners can resell approved products using negotiated wholesale discounts and markups. AWS Marketplace listing fees are published separately; for standard SaaS public offers the listing fee is 3%, while CPPO arrangements add 0.5% to the listing fee. This is not an AgentOS commission rate, but demonstrates a formal route for reseller economics.

Sources: https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/apn/amazon-bedrock-now-available-for-partner-resale/ ; https://docs.aws.amazon.com/marketplace/latest/userguide/channel-partner-offers.html ; https://docs.aws.amazon.com/marketplace/latest/userguide/listing-fees.html

### Cursor / coding agents

Cursor's referral program existed in a limited rollout in May 2026: new users received 50% off the first month and referrers received $25 usage credit per paid referral, capped at 10 rewards per cycle. Cursor support/community posts in July/August 2026 state that the referral program has since been discontinued. Therefore **do not build current revenue projections around Cursor referrals**. Cursor has begun a broader partner strategy and may be a future enterprise partner target.

Sources: Cursor community/support reports and Cursor's July 2026 Benchmark Partners announcement: https://cursor.com/blog/benchmarkpartners

## Agent/AI-tool affiliate opportunity

There is a meaningful second category beyond foundation-model vendors: AI applications and agents that have explicit affiliate programs. Current affiliate tracking identifies active or potentially active programs across categories such as AI productivity, research, coding, media, automation and agent tools. However, commission rates often require application and are not always publicly disclosed.

Examples worth investigating for direct partnership include AI research, coding, agent, media and automation products. AgentOS should maintain a live partner registry rather than hard-code commission rates.

## Australian compliance

AgentOS should clearly disclose commercial relationships and affiliate incentives. The ACCC states that Australian Consumer Law applies to social media and online promotions and that businesses must not make false or misleading claims. The ACCC has specifically taken enforcement action where influencer relationships were not disclosed. Commercial recommendations in AgentOS should therefore identify when AgentOS may receive a commission and should remain capability-driven rather than ranking providers solely by commission.

Sources: https://www.accc.gov.au/business/advertising-and-promotions ; https://www.accc.gov.au/business/advertising-and-promotions/social-media-promotions ; https://www.accc.gov.au/media-release/photobookshop-pays-penalties-for-influencer-reviews

## Recommended commercial architecture

Separate three registries:

### 1. Model Registry

Tracks technical capability:
- provider
- model
- context
- reasoning
- coding
- vision
- tool use
- agent support
- availability
- pricing
- free/limited availability
- last verified

### 2. Provider Entitlement Registry

Tracks user/provider access:
- provider account/API key
- subscription connection
- quota
- spend
- permission
- region
- billing source
- user authorisation

### 3. Partner Revenue Registry

Tracks commercial opportunity:
- provider/agent
- relationship type: affiliate/referral/partner/reseller/marketplace
- eligibility by country
- application status
- commission type
- commission percentage/amount
- recurring vs one-off
- cookie/attribution period
- minimum payout
- API/referral attribution
- terms URL
- last verified
- confidence level
- owner/contact

## Revenue strategy recommendation

Do not assume every major AI provider will offer a public affiliate percentage. The most valuable opportunities may be formal partner, referral, deal-registration, reseller or marketplace arrangements rather than consumer affiliate links.

Priority order for AgentOS:

1. **OpenAI Partner Network** — strategic application/partner discussion.
2. **Anthropic Claude Partner Network** — strategic partner/referral/deal-registration discussion.
3. **AWS Partner / Bedrock resale + AWS Marketplace** — strongest formal multi-model channel opportunity identified.
4. **Google Cloud Partner Network** — investigate Australian partner route; the public affiliate program is currently US/Canada only.
5. **Perplexity** — investigate current channel/referral program for business/enterprise.
6. **Groq** — verify current affiliate/partner status directly before relying on it.
7. **OpenRouter** — request direct confirmation of any partner/referral economics.
8. **AI agent/application vendors** — build a curated affiliate portfolio where rates and terms are confirmed.

## Forecasting rule

For business-plan modelling:

- Confirmed public commission = may be modelled.
- Partner program with unpublished/negotiated economics = model as scenario, not guaranteed revenue.
- Third-party-only claim = do not include in base case.
- Program unavailable/closed = $0 in base case.

## Strategic conclusion

The AgentOS business should not depend on affiliate commissions from any one model vendor. The durable value is the AgentOS orchestration layer. Partner/affiliate/API revenue should be an additional monetisation stream that grows as AgentOS becomes the place where users discover, connect, compare and operate multiple AI models and agents.

This research should be revalidated before commercial launch because provider programs, regional eligibility, model availability, quotas and commission terms can change quickly.

# AgentOS Affiliate Program — Commercial Model Recommendation — 2026-09-12

**Owner:** Marketing Overseer  
**Status:** RECOMMENDATION / OWNER APPROVAL REQUIRED  
**Scope:** Creator/Affiliate Program only. Member Referral remains a separate program.

## Executive recommendation

Launch with:

- **25% commission on qualifying net revenue during the customer's first 12 months**;
- **90-day attribution window**;
- for the current annual plans, this means 25% of the first annual payment;
- if a customer buys Co-worker and upgrades to Operator within 90 days of the original paid conversion, pay 25% on the incremental qualifying net revenue from that upgrade;
- no commission on renewals after the first 12 months at launch;
- **30% invited performance tier** for proven high-performing, compliant creators;
- 30-day validation/hold after the underlying transaction becomes commissionable, with payout on the next regular payout cycle;
- commissions calculated on **net revenue actually retained**, excluding tax, refunds, chargebacks, credits, fraudulent/self-referred transactions and other excluded amounts defined in the Terms.

This is the recommended launch model because it is simple, creator-friendly and aligned with the current $39 / $99 annual pricing while limiting open-ended commission liability.

## Why not say “25% recurring for 12 months” without clarification

AgentOS currently uses annual pricing. On an annual $99 Operator purchase there is only one annual payment inside the first 12 months. Therefore “25% recurring for 12 months” can sound like multiple payments even though the current customer payment schedule does not create them.

Recommended public wording:

> Earn 25% of qualifying net revenue from customers you refer during their first 12 months with AgentOS.

Then clarify:

> With annual billing, that normally means 25% of the customer's first annual payment, plus qualifying upgrade revenue where applicable.

If monthly billing is introduced later, the same 25%-for-first-12-months rule can operate across monthly subscription payments without rewriting the program logic.

## Current plan economics at 25%

### Co-worker — $39/year

Gross affiliate commission before exclusions/refunds: **$9.75**.

### Operator — $99/year

Gross affiliate commission before exclusions/refunds: **$24.75**.

### Co-worker → Operator upgrade

Nominal annual price difference: **$60**.

If the qualifying upgrade occurs inside the 90-day upgrade window, additional affiliate commission at 25%: **$15.00**.

Total creator commission across the initial $39 purchase plus qualifying $60 upgrade: **$24.75**, matching the commission that would have been payable on an initial $99 Operator purchase.

This removes an incentive for creators to avoid recommending Co-worker to genuinely uncertain users merely because Operator pays more commission.

## Alternative commission options considered

### 20% — conservative

- $39 Co-worker: $7.80
- $99 Operator: $19.80
- $60 qualifying upgrade: $12.00

**Assessment:** financially conservative, but less compelling for creators given the low annual ticket price.

### 25% — recommended

- $39 Co-worker: $9.75
- $99 Operator: $24.75
- $60 qualifying upgrade: $15.00

**Assessment:** strong enough to be meaningful without requiring permanent recurring liability.

### 30% — aggressive/default not recommended

- $39 Co-worker: $11.70
- $99 Operator: $29.70
- $60 qualifying upgrade: $18.00

**Assessment:** useful as an earned/invited performance tier, but unnecessarily expensive as the universal starting rate until conversion quality, refund rate, support cost and retained customer value are measured.

## Recommended high-performer tier

**30% of qualifying net revenue during the first 12 months** by invitation only.

Eligibility should consider:

- sustained qualified sales volume;
- low refund/chargeback rate;
- compliance record;
- quality of audience/customer fit;
- accurate product representation;
- low support burden / low misleading-claim rate;
- strategic reach or exceptional content value.

Do not publish a guaranteed volume threshold until program economics are measured. AgentOS should be able to invite creators into the tier rather than automatically triggering it from raw sales count alone.

## Attribution recommendation

### Base rule

**90-day cookie / eligible attribution window.**

Recommended attribution rule:

- last eligible affiliate referral before the first qualifying paid conversion wins;
- internal AgentOS navigation must not overwrite valid affiliate attribution;
- self-referrals, cookie stuffing, forced clicks, unauthorized coupon interception and attribution hijacking are invalid;
- manual/coupon attribution should only be used where explicitly supported by the tracking system and Terms.

### Upgrade rule

If a referred customer initially purchases Co-worker and upgrades to Operator **within 90 days of the original paid conversion**, the same affiliate receives 25% of qualifying incremental net upgrade revenue, provided the account/transaction remains attributable and valid.

Upgrades after that period are not commissionable at launch unless AgentOS later adopts a longer customer-affiliate relationship model.

## Renewals recommendation

**Do not pay commissions on annual renewals beyond the first 12 months at launch.**

Reasons:

- keeps customer-acquisition cost bounded;
- simplifies accounting and disputes;
- avoids indefinite affiliate liability before LTV is known;
- lets AgentOS validate whether creator-referred customers retain differently from other customers;
- still gives a meaningful acquisition incentive at 25%.

This can be revisited later if measured retention/LTV supports a lifetime or multi-year model.

## Refunds, chargebacks and reversals

Commission should only vest on valid retained revenue.

Recommended rules:

- refunded transaction → associated commission reversed;
- partial refund → commission adjusted proportionally;
- chargeback/fraud → commission reversed;
- duplicate transaction → only the retained valid transaction counts;
- self-referral or prohibited attribution → no commission;
- if a payout has already occurred, AgentOS may offset valid reversals against future commissions, subject to fair-contract/legal review.

## Payout timing recommendation

Use a **30-day validation hold** from the date a commission becomes payable, then include it in the next scheduled payout cycle.

Recommended operating cadence:

- calculate monthly;
- pay monthly where threshold and payment details are satisfied;
- suggested minimum payout threshold: **AUD $50 or local/platform equivalent**, subject to payment-provider capability and legal/accounting review;
- balances below threshold roll forward.

The threshold is a recommendation, not yet approved.

## Customer discounts

Do not make a standing affiliate discount mandatory at launch.

Reason:

- AgentOS already has low annual pricing;
- stacking a discount and a 25% commission may create unnecessary margin pressure;
- creator value should initially come from trusted explanation and demonstrated utility, not discount-code competition.

If a campaign-specific customer incentive is later approved, it should be separately budgeted and attributed.

## Commission basis

Recommended definition of **Qualifying Net Revenue**:

Amount actually received and retained by AgentOS for an eligible purchase during the commissionable period, excluding where applicable:

- GST/VAT/sales taxes;
- refunds and credits;
- chargebacks;
- payment reversals;
- fraudulent transactions;
- self-referrals;
- unauthorized discounts;
- excluded add-ons or pass-through third-party charges specifically identified in the Commission Schedule.

Final tax/accounting wording requires professional review.

## Add-ons / BYO AI / third-party spend

Default recommendation:

- AgentOS software subscription revenue: commissionable according to schedule;
- direct pass-through API/model/provider charges: **not commissionable** unless explicitly listed;
- third-party products/services: not commissionable unless AgentOS is contractually authorized to share revenue and the schedule explicitly includes them;
- future first-party paid add-ons: individually classified in the Commission Schedule.

This prevents affiliates being promised commission on revenue AgentOS does not actually retain.

## Member Referral separation

The Member Referral Program should not use this commission structure.

Recommended member rewards remain non-cash/value-based initially, such as AgentOS account credit, temporary Operator capability, Night Shift allowance or similar governed benefits.

Affiliate = commercial promoter.  
Member referral = customer advocacy/reward.

## Launch recommendation

**Approve commercially, subject to legal/accounting review:**

> 25% of qualifying net revenue during the referred customer's first 12 months; 90-day attribution; qualifying Co-worker→Operator upgrade inside 90 days receives commission on incremental revenue; no renewal commission after first 12 months; invited high-performer tier at 30%.

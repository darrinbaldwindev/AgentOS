### FACT / RESEARCH CLAIMS
- Gemini reports that Google and Bing prohibit deceptive cloaking/doorway-style practices and that search optimization should not depend on crawler-only content.
- Gemini reports that structured information, clear entities, authoritative first-party information and technically accessible pages are relevant to search/AI discovery.

### VERIFIED BY GEMINI
- Gemini reported that the current AgentOS repository does not contain a production-ready public marketing website or dedicated production AEO pipeline.

### INFERENCE
- AI answer engines may benefit from semantically clear, directly extractable and authoritative content.
- Explicit developer use-case pages may capture higher-intent traffic than only broad category pages; this requires measurement.

### RECOMMENDATION
- Prepare a unified website architecture, potentially using a modern static/ISR framework, with canonical URLs, structured data, breadcrumbs and evidence-backed content.
- Define Search Intelligence as a native capability family governed by AgentOS Tier 2.3 controls.
- Build a small initial set of high-value pages before scaling content production.

### HYPOTHESIS
- Pages targeting explicit developer use cases such as local AI agents and agent budget controls may outperform broad category pages. Validate through actual search and conversion data.

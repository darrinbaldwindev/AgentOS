## Purpose
Record the strategic findings developed in the owner/ChatGPT Overseer conversation concerning Lenny's Product Pass, comparable developer/AI offers, affiliate opportunities, startup credits/perks, and their usefulness to AgentOS and ongoing project development.

## Key strategic findings

1. AgentOS must remain provider/model agnostic. The Overseer identity belongs to AgentOS, not to an AI provider. The model used for a task is a routing decision based on capability, entitlement, cost, availability and policy.
2. A paid AI subscription should not automatically become the permanent intelligence layer merely because it is paid. The Overseer should determine the appropriate model for each task, including free models when they satisfy requirements.
3. Commercial/affiliate relationships are an additional monetisation layer, not the basis for model selection. Recommendations must remain capability-driven and disclose commercial relationships.
4. Developer/productivity offers such as Lenny's Product Pass should be evaluated primarily for capability unlocked, development acceleration, cost avoided, and relevance to AgentOS; affiliate revenue is a secondary benefit.
5. AgentOS should maintain a live partner/revenue registry covering affiliate, referral, partner, reseller, marketplace and channel programs, with country eligibility, terms, attribution, commission type, confidence and last verification.
6. Startup credits and partner programs can materially reduce early development costs. These include relevant cloud, AI, developer tooling, startup and accelerator programs, subject to current eligibility and terms.
7. The existing AgentOS repository already contains a commercial AI partner research document and affiliate research infrastructure. New research should extend rather than duplicate those records.
